# 语法总结

> 只总结**我自己写错的**语法点。每条都有「我写的 ✗」和「正确 ✓」,后面标出处。

## 1. 词尾决定词性

| 词性 | 常见词尾 | 我写错的 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 形容词 | -able、-ible、-ing、-ed | ✗ acceptabled → ✓ acceptable;✗ unimaged → ✓ unimaginable;✗ development countries → ✓ developing countries;✗ the isolate people → ✓ the isolated people |
| 名词 | -ment、-tion、-ity | ✗ in employe → ✓ in employment;✗ unqueite → ✓ inequality |
| 过去分词 | -ed | ✗ has transformat → ✓ has transformed;✗ must be narrow → ✓ must be narrowed |

- 介词后面要名词:in **employment**。
- 形容词后面不加 -ed:**acceptable**。

出处:11847

## 2. 否定用前缀,不用 not + 动词

| 中文 | ✗ | ✓ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 不可避免的 | is not avoide | is **unavoidable** / **inevitable** |
| 不熟悉的 | unsimilar | **unfamiliar** |
| 不平等 | unqueite | **inequality** |
| 处于不利地位的 | disadvanted | **disadvantaged** |

注意:✗ `I disagree that the situation is not avoidable` 是双重否定,意思反了。✓ `I disagree that the situation is unavoidable`。

出处:11847

## 3. 被动语态:be + 过去分词

| ✗ | ✓ |
| --- | --- |
| the divide can be and must be **narrow** | the divide can be, and must be, **narrowed** |
| shouldn't be xxx | shouldn't be **tolerated** |
| should be **simplely** accepted | should be **simply** accepted / should **simply** be accepted |

副词放在 be 前面或后面都对;放在情态动词和 be 之间(should **simply** be accepted)更常见。这里的错误只是拼写。

出处:11847

## 4. make 的三种用法

| 结构 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| make + 宾语 + **形容词** | ✓ make remote work … **possible**(你用对了) |
| make + 人 + **动词原形** | make people **feel** isolated |
| 「使…无法」用 **prevent sb from doing** | ✗ make million people didn't get → ✓ **prevent** millions of people **from getting** these opportunities |

出处:11847

## 5. 介词后面用 doing

| ✗ | ✓ |
| --- | --- |
| via work at home | **by working** at home |
| contiune student | continue **studying** |
| to made benefit more share | **so that** the benefits **are shared** |

- by doing = 通过……的方式。
- continue + doing / to do。
- to 后面用动词原形(to make),不是 to made。

**by 后面接名词时要小心(11847-2):**

| 结构 | 意思 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| by + doing | 通过做某事 | kept their jobs **by working** at home ✓ |
| by + 交通 / 通讯方式(不加冠词) | 乘…、用… | by bus、by email、by phone |
| through / via + 名词 | 通过某个渠道 | continued studying **through** online courses(✗ by online courses) |

出处:11847-1、11847-2

## 6. 大作文的时态

| 用在哪里 | 时态 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 讨论现状 | 一般现在时 | many people **are** left behind;elderly people **are** put off(✗ were) |
| 到现在为止的变化 | 现在完成时 | technology **has transformed** our lives |
| 过去的例子 | 一般过去时 | during the pandemic, students **continued** … and employees **kept** …(✓ 你用对了) |
| 有过去时间 | 一般过去时 | all of which **were** unimaginable a generation ago(✗ is) |

出处:11847

## 7. 主谓一致

- the digital divide **is** threatening(✗ are)。divide 是单数。
- a significant proportion of people **remain**:proportion of + 复数名词,动词用复数也可以。

出处:11847

## 8. 中文语序 → 英文语序

中文把修饰语放在前面,英文常放在后面,用 of / with / who 连接。

| 中文 | ✗ 我写的 | ✓ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 与世界各地亲友的即时联系 | global friend and family of instant connec | instant contact **with** friends and family **around the world** |
| 一种新的社会不平等形式 | a new social unqueite form | a new form **of** social inequality |
| 不能上网的人 | the peopel who connect not | people **who are not connected** / those **without access** |
| 能使用的人 | people have right to access | people **who have access** |

**「在哪方面有优势」要紧跟 edge / advantage(11847-1、11847-2 都错了):**

✗ gain a genuine edge compared with people who are not connected **in employment, study and social life**
✓ gain a genuine edge **in employment, study and social life** compared with people who are not connected

放在句末,in employment … 会被读成修饰 people who are not connected(「在就业上没联网的人」)。修饰谁,就紧跟谁。

出处:11847-1、11847-2

## 9. 关系代词不能省

| ✗ | ✓ |
| --- | --- |
| people have right to access | people **who** have access |
| …, which is unimaged a generation ago(前面有 4 样东西) | …, **all of which** were unimaginable a generation ago |

- 作主语的 who 不能省。
- 指前面多样东西,用 **all of which** + 复数动词。

出处:11847

## 10. 「随着」用 As

✗ `Over the daily life gradually moving forward internet`
✓ `**As** daily life gradually **moves online**`

As + 完整的句子(主语 + 动词)。over 只用于时间段:over the ten-year period。

出处:11847

## 11. 「不应……而应……」

| ✗ | ✓ |
| --- | --- |
| shouldn't regard it as xx by-product, ranther invest … | shouldn't regard it as an inevitable by-product; **instead, they should** invest … |
| | **Rather than regarding** it as an inevitable by-product, they should invest … |

rather 不能单独连接两个分句;用 instead,或 rather than + doing 放句首。

**instead 是副词,不是连词(11847-2 又错):** 两个完整的句子不能只用逗号连接。

| ✗ | ✓ |
| --- | --- |
| …shouldn't regard it as inevitable by-product**, instead,** they should … | …shouldn't regard it as an inevitable by-product**;** instead, they should … |
| | …shouldn't regard it as an inevitable by-product**.** Instead, they should … |

同类的副词:however、therefore、moreover。前面要用句号或分号,不能用逗号。

出处:11847-1、11847-2

## 12. 冠词和单复数

| ✗ | ✓ | 规则 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| while signiciant proportion | while **a** significant proportion | a significant proportion of 固定搭配 |
| Smartphone and internet | **Smartphones** and **the** internet | the internet 要加 the;泛指的可数名词用复数 |
| millions students / million people | millions **of** students / millions **of** people | millions of + 复数 |
| the live of many people | the **lives** of many people | 每个人的生活:lives |
| Smartphones and internet(11847-2 又错) | Smartphones and **the** internet | **the internet 永远加 the** |
| Governments and business | Governments and **businesses** | 指企业(可数),和 governments 一样用复数 |
| regard it as inevitable by-product | regard it as **an** inevitable by-product | 单数可数名词前要冠词;inevitable 元音开头用 an |

出处:11847-1、11847-2

## 14. 动词 + 介词搭配

| ✗ | ✓ |
| --- | --- |
| invest basic infrastructure(11847-1、11847-2 都错) | invest **in** basic infrastructure |

invest 后面直接跟的是**钱**:invest money **in** sth。投资某个领域,要用 invest **in**。

出处:11847-1、11847-2

## 13. 标点和大小写

- **I** 永远大写(✗ i firmly、i don't)。
- 英文没有顿号「、」,列举用逗号:remote work, online education, remote medicine。
- 逗号紧跟前一个词,后面加空格:✗ `course ,and` → ✓ `courses, and`。
- 句首字母大写:✗ `governemnt and business` → ✓ `Governments and businesses`。
- **句号、逗号后面一定空一格(11847-2 漏了 5 处):** ✗ `advantages.Smartphones`、`pandemic,millions` → ✓ `advantages. Smartphones`、`pandemic, millions`。
- 句号前面不空格,也不要连续两个空格:✗ `working at home .` → ✓ `working at home.`。

出处:11847-1、11847-2
