## 题目

Some people suggest that a country should try to produce all the food for its population and import as little food as possible. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:想法出于好意(价格稳定、就业),但对很多国家来说,粮食完全自给不现实。

## 中文(用来翻译)

随着人口增长,如何养活一个国家的人已经成为一个主要问题。有人建议,一个国家应该生产其人口所需的全部食物,并尽可能少进口食物。我不同意这一观点,因为对大多数国家来说这不现实。

诚然,实现粮食自给自足的想法是出于好意。通过在国内生产足够的食物,一个国家可以保持食品价格稳定,因为进口食物往往涉及国际运输费用和税费。此外,增加国内生产能创造更多就业机会,这对于依赖农业的国家尤其重要,比如印度、巴西和墨西哥。

然而,对许多国家来说,为全体人口生产足够的食物即使不是不可能,也是不现实的。由于地理和气候条件,一些国家根本没有足够的耕地来种植粮食。例如,新加坡是一个面积小、人口密集的城市国家,90% 以上的食物都要进口;许多位于沙漠地区的国家也几乎没有适合耕种的土地。再以格陵兰岛为例。岛上大部分地区被冰覆盖,只有不到 1% 的土地可以用于生产食物。生活在这些地方的人将永远需要依赖进口食物。而且,即使是耕地充足的国家也能从贸易中受益,因为进口热带水果或咖啡比在寒冷的气候里尝试种植便宜得多。

总之,我认为一个国家不应该试图生产自己所需的全部食物或限制进口,因为完全自给自足不是一个现实的目标。在全球化的世界里,各国在粮食方面将始终相互依赖。

As populations grow, how to feed a country's people has become a major concern. Some people suggest that a country should produce all the food its population needs and import as little food as possible. I disagree with this view, because it is not practical for most countries.

Admittedly, the idea of becoming self-sufficient in food comes from good intentions. By producing enough food at home, a country can keep food prices stable, since imported food often involves international transport costs and taxes. In addition, increasing domestic production creates more jobs, which is especially important for countries that rely on agriculture, such as India, Brazil and Mexico.

However, producing enough food for the whole population would be impractical, if not impossible, for many countries. Because of their geography and climate, some countries simply do not have enough farmland to grow food. Singapore, for example, is a small and crowded city-state that imports more than 90% of its food, and many countries in desert regions have little land suitable for farming. Take Greenland as another example. Most of the island is covered by ice, so less than 1% of its land can be used for food production. People living in these places will always have to rely on imported food. Moreover, even countries with plenty of farmland benefit from trade, since importing tropical fruit or coffee is far cheaper than trying to grow them in a cold climate.

In conclusion, I believe a country should not try to produce all its own food or restrict imports, because complete self-sufficiency is not a realistic goal. In a globalised world, countries will always depend on each other for food.
