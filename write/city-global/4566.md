## 题目

More and more people move into cities, so some governments replace farmland and parks near cities with more houses. Is this a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 总体是负面的:能快速提供住房,但损害粮食供应、公众健康和野生动物。

## 中文(用来翻译)

近年来,许多人从农村搬到城市,寻求更好的生活水平。为了安置他们,一些政府在城市附近的农田和公园上建造新房。虽然这有助于解决住房短缺问题,但我认为这在很大程度上是一个负面的发展。

主要的好处是它能快速提供更多住房。随着城市扩张,住房变得稀缺而昂贵,在附近的土地上建房往往是满足需求最快的办法。这样,新居民就能住在离城市近的地方,方便地获得工作、学校、医院和娱乐设施,既节省时间,又提高了生活质量。

然而,这一发展有严重的弊端。首先,失去农田会减少城市附近能种植的粮食,于是更多的食物要从远处运来,这会增加成本和污染。其次,公园是城市居民为数不多可以放松和锻炼的绿地之一。当公园被住房取代时,居民就失去了躲开噪音和压力的地方,这会损害他们的身心健康。最后,农田和绿地的破坏也毁掉了许多鸟类和动物的自然栖息地,那些无法适应城市扩张的物种可能会从当地消失。

总之,虽然在农田和公园上建房可以缓解住房短缺,但我认为它对粮食供应、公众健康和野生动物的负面影响更大。政府应该更好地利用现有的城市土地,比如重新开发旧工业区。

In recent years, many people have moved from the countryside to cities in search of a better standard of living. To house them, some governments have built new homes on farmland and parks near cities. Although this helps solve the housing shortage, I believe it is a largely negative development.

The main advantage is that it provides more homes quickly. As cities grow, housing becomes scarce and expensive, and building on nearby land is often the fastest way to meet the demand. New residents can then live close to the city, where they have easy access to jobs, schools, hospitals and entertainment, which saves them time and improves their quality of life.

However, this development has serious drawbacks. First, losing farmland reduces the amount of food that can be grown near cities, so more food has to be transported from far away, which raises costs and pollution. Second, parks are among the few green spaces where city dwellers can relax and exercise. When they are replaced by housing, residents lose places to escape from noise and stress, which can harm their physical and mental health. Finally, the destruction of fields and green areas also destroys natural habitats for many birds and animals, and species that cannot adapt to the expanding city may disappear from the area.

In conclusion, although building houses on farmland and parks can ease the housing shortage, I believe its negative effects on food supply, public health and wildlife are greater. Governments should instead make better use of existing urban land, for example by redeveloping old industrial sites.
