## 题目

Nowadays, it is easier and more affordable for people to visit other countries. Is it a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 总体是积极的:促进文化理解和经济增长,但环境代价需要更多关注。

## 中文(用来翻译)

得益于更便宜的机票、更开放的边境和不断提高的生活水平,出国旅行对许多人来说似乎不再是一种奢侈。我认为,总体而言这是一个积极的发展,尽管它的环境代价不应被忽视。

从积极的方面看,出国旅行促进了文化理解和经济增长。对游客来说,去世界其他地方看看、体验异国文化能开阔视野,让他们享受不同生活方式带来的新鲜感,并更加了解家乡以外的世界。更重要的是,许多国家从外国游客身上获得了可观的收入。这对发展中地区来说尤其如此,在那里旅游业占年收入的很大一部分。例如,马尔代夫和阿鲁巴这样的地方就严重依赖旅游业,旅游业约占马尔代夫经济的四分之一。

然而,出国旅行也给环境带来严重的风险。不受控制的旅游威胁着许多自然区域,导致土壤侵蚀、废物排入海洋、濒危物种失去栖息地等问题。随着游客去寻找越来越偏远的目的地,比如南极洲,这些风险还会增加。威尼斯这样的热门城市也饱受游客过多之苦,这既破坏环境,也影响当地居民的生活。此外,旅游业对环境的影响远远超出热门目的地本身,因为航空、海运和陆路交通都会产生大量碳排放。

总之,出国旅行变得更容易这一趋势显然给游客和目的地国家都带来了好处,但它对环境的破坏需要得到更多公众关注。希望随着意识的提高,能出台有效的措施,让全球旅游业可持续发展。

International travel no longer seems a luxury for many people, thanks to cheaper flights, more open borders and rising living standards. In my view, this is a positive development overall, although its environmental costs should not be ignored.

On the positive side, international travel promotes cultural understanding and economic growth. For tourists, visiting other parts of the world and experiencing foreign cultures opens up new perspectives, allowing them to enjoy the novelty of a different way of life and become more aware of the world beyond their home country. More importantly, many countries earn considerable income from international visitors. This is particularly true for developing regions, where tourism makes up a large share of annual income. For instance, places like the Maldives and Aruba rely heavily on the tourism industry, which accounts for roughly a quarter of the Maldives' economy.

However, international travel also poses serious risks to the environment. Uncontrolled tourism threatens many natural areas, leading to problems such as soil erosion, waste discharged into the sea and the loss of habitats for endangered species. These risks will grow as tourists seek out ever more remote destinations, such as Antarctica. Popular cities such as Venice also suffer from overcrowding, which harms both the environment and the lives of local residents. In addition, the environmental impact of tourism goes far beyond popular destinations themselves, because air, sea and land transport all produce large amounts of carbon emissions.

In conclusion, the trend of easier international travel clearly brings benefits to both travellers and destination countries, but its environmental damage needs more public attention. Hopefully, as awareness grows, effective measures can be introduced to make global tourism sustainable.
