## 题目

The spread of multinational companies and the resulting increase in globalization will have positive effects on everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:跨国公司带来经济和文化好处,但不是每个人都受益(当地环境受损、发达国家工人失业)。

## 中文(用来翻译)

在过去几十年里,跨国公司的扩张以及由此带来的全球化,给世界上几乎每一个国家都带来了巨大的变化。许多人欢迎这一趋势,因为它带来了机会和经济增长。尽管它有明显的好处,但我不同意每个人都从中受益。

跨国公司的积极影响主要体现在经济和文化方面。对于欠发达地区来说,低廉的劳动力成本是国际公司去那里投资的重要原因,这促进了当地生产,提高了人们的收入。例如,许多知名公司已经在东南亚和非洲设立了工厂和分公司,这些地区的经济在近几十年里快速增长。此外,这些公司还促进了文化交流,因为它们把自己的产品、价值观和工作方式带到了新的国家,这有助于减少文化隔阂。

然而,并不是每个人都能从这一趋势中获益。跨国公司扩张到新地区时,往往会过度开发当地资源,造成空气和水污染等环境问题。结果,当地人的生活条件可能会变差。同时,在劳动力成本较高的发达国家,当生产转移到更便宜的国家时,普通工人可能会失业。例如,随着制造业转移到亚洲,美国和英国的许多工厂工人失去了工作,其中一些人指责全球化夺走了他们的生计。

总之,虽然我承认全球化的积极影响,但我认为它并没有让每个人都受益。我们需要付出更多努力来解决它造成的环境和社会问题。

Over the past few decades, the spread of multinational companies and the resulting globalisation have brought dramatic changes to almost every country in the world. Many people welcome this trend because of the opportunities and economic growth it brings. Despite its obvious benefits, I do not agree that everyone has benefited from it.

The positive effects of multinational companies are mainly economic and cultural. For less developed regions, low labour costs are a strong reason for international companies to invest there, which boosts local production and raises people's incomes. For example, many well-known companies have set up factories and branches in Southeast Asia and Africa, where economies have grown rapidly in recent decades. In addition, these companies contribute to cultural exchange, because they bring their products, values and ways of working to new countries, which can help reduce cultural barriers.

However, not everyone gains from this trend. When multinational companies expand into new regions, they often exploit local resources, causing environmental problems such as air and water pollution. As a result, the living conditions of local people may get worse. Meanwhile, in developed countries where labour costs are higher, ordinary workers may lose their jobs when production moves to cheaper countries. For instance, many factory workers in the United States and Britain have lost their jobs as manufacturing has shifted to Asia, and some of them blame globalisation for taking away their livelihoods.

In conclusion, while I recognise the positive effects of globalisation, I believe it does not benefit everyone. More effort is needed to deal with the environmental and social problems it causes.
