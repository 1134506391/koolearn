## 题目

Some people think certain old buildings are worth preserving more than the other ones. Which types of old buildings should be preserved? Do you think the advantages of preserving old buildings outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 只保护两类老建筑(有历史价值的、有民族特色和审美价值的);除此之外,大规模保护弊大于利。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为没有必要保护所有的历史建筑。在这篇文章中,我将说明哪些类型的老建筑值得保护,并解释为什么我认为大规模保护老建筑弊大于利。

一般来说,有两类老建筑需要保护。第一类是具有特殊历史价值的建筑。如果这些建筑得到很好的维护,城市的文化底蕴就能得到加强,人们也能记住自己的历史。第二类是具有民族特色和审美价值的老建筑。这些建筑有助于保持文化多样性,构成城市景观的一部分,还能为艺术家提供灵感来源。

然而,保护老建筑是否利大于弊经常引起争论。我认为,保护过多的老建筑会带来几个问题。虽然这些建筑可以吸引游客、促进当地经济,但它们带来的收入通常远远少于政府在资金、时间和人力上的投入,这加重了政府的财政负担。事实上,促进经济增长还有很多其他办法。此外,如果一座城市有太多老建筑,它就很难发展。一方面,规划者可能很难修建现代化的道路和设施;另一方面,这座城市对需要现代化办公楼和工厂的外来投资者吸引力较低。

总之,具有重大历史意义的建筑,以及具有民族特色和审美价值的建筑,应该得到保护。但除此之外,保护老建筑弊大于利,因为它给政府带来沉重的财政负担,并阻碍城市的发展。

Some people claim that it is unnecessary to protect all historical buildings. In this essay, I will suggest which types of old buildings deserve to be preserved, and explain why I think the disadvantages of preserving old buildings on a large scale outweigh the advantages.

In general, two types of old buildings need to be protected. The first type is buildings with special historical value. If these buildings are well maintained, a city's cultural heritage can be strengthened, and people can remember their history. The second type is old buildings with ethnic character and aesthetic value. These buildings help maintain cultural diversity, form part of the city's landscape and provide artists with a source of inspiration.

However, whether the advantages of preserving old buildings outweigh the disadvantages is often debated, and in my opinion, preserving too many of them causes several problems. Although these buildings can attract tourists and boost the local economy, the income they generate is usually far less than what the government spends on them in money, time and labour, which increases the financial burden on the government. In fact, there are many other ways to promote economic growth. Moreover, it is difficult for a city to develop if it has too many old buildings. On the one hand, planners may find it hard to build modern roads and facilities; on the other hand, the city may be less attractive to outside investors who need modern offices and factories.

In conclusion, buildings of great historical importance and those with ethnic and aesthetic value should be preserved. Apart from these, however, the drawbacks of preserving old buildings outweigh the benefits, because it places a heavy financial burden on the government and holds back a city's development.
