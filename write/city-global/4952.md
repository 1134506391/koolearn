## 题目

In many cities, planners build shops, schools, offices and homes in specific areas which may be separated from each other. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 利大于弊:城市布局更好理解、基础设施利用更高效;弊端是交通拥堵和通勤距离长。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今,许多城市的规划者把购物中心、学校、办公楼和住宅建在不同的区域。我个人认为,这种做法利大于弊。

主要的好处是,功能分区让城市布局更容易理解。当每个区域都有明确的用途时,居民和游客都能很容易地找到要去的地方。例如,游客拿着一张清晰的城市地图,就能很快找到购物区或商务中心,这让在城市里出行更高效、更愉快。

另一个好处是,基础设施可以得到更有效的利用。例如,如果住宅集中在居住区,而不是分散在城市各处,那么建在那里的体育设施、公园和诊所就能服务街区里的每一个人。此外,这些设施集中在一起时,维护成本也更低。

然而,这种做法的弊端也不能忽视。当许多功能相同的建筑集中在一个区域时,它们往往会在一天中的同一时间段产生大量交通。例如,在清晨和傍晚,家长接送孩子时,学校门口聚集了很多车辆,这经常造成交通堵塞。此外,由于住宅离办公室和商店很远,许多居民每天都要长途出行,既浪费时间,又增加了汽车的使用。

总之,我想重申,把城市划分为不同功能的区域利大于弊,因为这让城市更容易理解,也让人们能充分利用基础设施。

Nowadays, planners in many cities build shopping centres, schools, offices and homes in separate areas. Personally, I believe that the advantages of this approach outweigh the disadvantages.

The main benefit is that functional zoning makes the city layout easier to understand. When each area has a clear purpose, both residents and visitors can easily find where they need to go. A visitor with a clear city map, for example, can quickly locate the shopping district or the business centre, which makes travelling around the city more efficient and enjoyable.

Another advantage is that infrastructure can be used more efficiently. For example, if homes are concentrated in residential districts rather than scattered across the city, the sports facilities, parks and clinics built there can serve everyone in the neighbourhood. In addition, maintaining these facilities costs less when they are grouped together.

However, the disadvantages of this approach cannot be ignored. When many buildings with the same function are clustered in one area, they tend to generate heavy traffic at the same times of day. For example, many cars gather outside school gates in the early morning and late afternoon when parents drop off and pick up their children, which often causes traffic jams. In addition, because homes are far from offices and shops, many residents have to travel long distances every day, which wastes time and increases car use.

In conclusion, I would like to reiterate that dividing a city into areas with different functions brings more benefits than drawbacks, because it makes the city easier to understand and helps people make the most of its infrastructure.
