## 题目

The restoration of old buildings in major cities in the world costs numerous governments' expenditure. This money should be used in new housing and road development. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 部分不同意:修复老建筑能保护文化、带动旅游经济;但建房修路也需要资金。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,政府花在维护老建筑上的钱应该用来建新住房和修路。我个人部分不同意这个观点,下面我将解释原因。

首先,资助老建筑很重要,因为这样可以保存艺术、文化和历史。许多老建筑具有艺术、文化和历史价值,但如果得不到保护,甚至被拆除,这些价值就会消失。有了政府的支持,这些建筑可以得到很好的维护,本地人和游客都能欣赏它们,它们还可以作为后代了解过去的场所。

此外,如果有足够的资金用于保护老建筑,当地经济就能受益。现代游客很少会被没有特色的地方吸引。然而,维护良好的老建筑可以成为热门旅游景点。因此,城市不仅能从门票中赚钱,还能从游客在住宿、餐饮和纪念品上的花费中获益。

另一方面,在新住房和道路上花钱也很有必要,因为它们能改善市民的居住条件和生活质量。有了更多新的公寓楼,人们因人口增长而面临的住房短缺就会减少。同时,更多的道路能给司机提供不同的路线到达目的地,这可以缓解交通拥堵。

总之,我部分不同意把政府资金从保护历史建筑中挪走,因为忽视它们会导致艺术、文化和历史的丧失,还会阻碍当地经济发展。不过,我承认,为了市民的日常生活,新住房和道路也值得投入资金。

Some people argue that government money spent on maintaining old buildings should instead be used to build new housing and roads. Personally, I partly disagree with this idea, and I will explain my reasons below.

First, funding old buildings is important because it preserves art, culture and history. Many old buildings have artistic, cultural and historical value, but without protection, or even after being demolished, this value is lost. With government support, these buildings can be well maintained, so that both locals and tourists can appreciate them, and they can also serve as places where future generations learn about the past.

In addition, if sufficient funds are given to preserve old buildings, the local economy can benefit. Modern travellers are rarely attracted to places with no distinctive character. Well-maintained old buildings, however, can become popular tourist attractions. Consequently, cities can earn money not only from admission fees but also from tourists' spending on accommodation, food and souvenirs.

On the other hand, it is also essential to spend money on new housing and roads, because they improve citizens' living conditions and quality of life. With more new apartment blocks, people will face fewer housing shortages caused by population growth. At the same time, more roads give drivers different routes to their destinations, which can ease traffic congestion.

In conclusion, I partly disagree that government funds should be diverted from preserving historical buildings, because neglecting them can lead to the loss of art, culture and history, and can also hold back the local economy. However, I accept that new housing and roads also deserve funding for the benefit of citizens' daily lives.
