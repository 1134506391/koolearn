## 题目

When a new town is planned, it is more important to develop public parks and sports facilities than shopping centres for people to spend their free time. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 完全同意:公园和体育设施让生活更丰富、更健康,还能增强社区归属感。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,在规划新城镇时,建造供人们休闲的公共公园和体育设施比建购物中心更重要。我完全同意这个观点,因为公园和体育设施能帮助人们过上有趣、健康的生活,并增强他们对当地社区的归属感。

首先,公园和体育设施比购物中心更能提高人们的生活质量。公园提供了开展各种活动的场所,丰富了人们的生活,而体育设施则帮助人们保持健康。例如,在公园里,人们可以下棋、玩飞盘或遛狗。同时,方便地使用体育设施让居民锻炼起来很方便,帮助他们养成规律锻炼的习惯。

其次,这些公共空间有助于建立良好的邻里关系和强烈的社区意识。当人们晚饭后在公园散步,或在当地的体育中心锻炼时,他们就有机会认识住在附近的人,交到朋友。同时,在公园里举办的社区活动,比如节日庆典和慈善跑,会让居民感到自己是这个群体的一员,从而增强他们的归属感。

总之,我完全同意在新城镇建公共公园和体育设施比建购物中心更重要。它们鼓励人们过积极健康的生活,拉近邻里之间的距离,形成强烈的社区意识,这些都是购物中心无法提供的。此外,由于现在人们可以在网上买很多东西,大型购物中心已经不像以前那样必要了。

Some people argue that when a new town is planned, it is more important to build public parks and sports facilities than shopping centres for people to enjoy in their free time. I totally agree with this idea, because parks and sports facilities help people lead interesting and healthy lives and strengthen their sense of belonging to the local community.

Firstly, parks and sports facilities improve people's quality of life more than shopping centres do. Parks provide places for a wide variety of activities, which enrich people's lives, while sports facilities help people stay healthy. In parks, for example, people can play chess, throw a Frisbee or walk their dogs. At the same time, easy access to sports facilities makes it convenient for residents to work out, helping them develop a regular exercise habit.

Secondly, these public spaces help build good relationships between neighbours and a strong sense of community. When people take a walk in the park after dinner or exercise at a local sports centre, they have opportunities to meet others who live nearby and make friends. Meanwhile, community events held in parks, such as festivals and charity runs, make residents feel that they are part of a group, which strengthens their sense of belonging.

In conclusion, I completely agree that it is more important to build public parks and sports facilities than shopping centres in a new town. They encourage people to live active and healthy lives, bring neighbours closer together and create a strong sense of community, which shopping centres cannot provide. Besides, since people can now buy many things online, large shopping centres are less necessary than they once were.
