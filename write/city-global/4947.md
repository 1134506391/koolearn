## 题目

Too much money is wasted on repairing old buildings that should be used to knock them down and build new ones. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 部分同意:有历史价值的老建筑要保护,普通老建筑应该拆了重建。

## 中文(用来翻译)

旧的市政厅、豪宅和商业街按现代标准来看可能浪费空间,而且维护费用往往很高。因此,有人认为,花在修缮这些建筑上的钱应该用来把它们拆掉、建新的。我部分同意:有历史价值的建筑值得保留,但许多普通的老建筑应该被替换。

一方面,保护某些老建筑,特别是那些被认定为文化遗产的建筑,显然是值得的。主要原因是,这些建筑常常是举行宗教、政治或文化活动的地方,是历史的活见证。在一座维护良好的历史建筑里跟着导游参观,能让本地人和游客都感受到与过去的联系,这足以证明保护费用是值得的。这些地方还能带来经济效益。它们精美的材料、装饰性的外立面和独特的玻璃工艺让它们成为热门旅游景点,为地方政府和商家提供了重要的收入来源。

另一方面,许多其他老建筑的价值要小得多,应该让位于重新开发。最常见的理由是城市空间不足,无法容纳快速增长的人口。得益于设计和建造技术的进步,高层建筑已被证明是满足住宅和商业需求更具成本效益的方式。还应该认识到,许多老建筑当初只是为了实用而建,并不是为了使用几百年。经过几十年的高强度使用,它们的结构可能严重老化,反复维修的费用太高,难以为继。

总之,并不是所有老建筑都值得投入大量资金去维护和修复。虽然对少数具有历史和文化意义的建筑来说,这笔开支是合理的,但当现代建筑在许多方面明显更好时,保留其他可能不安全的老住宅和商业建筑就没有什么意义了。

Old town halls, mansions and shopping arcades can be space-inefficient by modern standards and are often expensive to maintain. Some people therefore argue that the money spent on repairing them should instead be used to knock them down and build new ones. I partly agree: buildings of historical value deserve to be preserved, but many ordinary old buildings should be replaced.

On the one hand, preserving certain old buildings, especially those recognised as heritage sites, is clearly worthwhile. The main reason is that these buildings, where religious, political or cultural events were often held, are living evidence of history. A guided tour through a well-maintained historic building gives both locals and tourists a sense of connection to the past, which more than justifies the cost of conservation. These sites also bring economic benefits. Their beautiful materials, decorative fronts and unusual glasswork make them popular tourist attractions, which provide an important source of income for local councils and businesses.

On the other hand, many other old buildings have far less value and should give way to redevelopment. The most common reason is the shortage of urban space to house rapidly growing populations. Thanks to advances in design and construction, high-rise buildings have proved to be a more cost-effective way to meet residential and commercial needs. It should also be recognised that many old buildings were built simply to be functional, not to last for centuries. After decades of heavy use, their structure can deteriorate seriously, and repeated repairs become too costly to be sustainable.

In conclusion, not all old buildings deserve large amounts of money for maintenance and restoration. While this spending is justified for selected sites of historical and cultural importance, there is little point in keeping other old housing and commercial buildings that may be unsafe, when modern buildings are clearly better in many ways.
