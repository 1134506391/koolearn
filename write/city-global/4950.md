## 题目

More and more people move from the countryside to big cities. Does this development bring more advantages or disadvantages to the environment? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 弊大于利:空气污染、生物多样性受威胁;好处是城市人均资源消耗更低,也推动了绿色技术。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今,越来越多的农村居民离开家乡,搬到大城市。我个人认为,这一趋势对环境的弊大于利。

主要的弊端是大城市空气质量恶化。随着越来越多的人搬到这些地方定居,他们中的许多人可能会购买严重依赖化石燃料的私家车。燃烧这些燃料会释放大量有害气体,比如二氧化氮,结果空气污染就成了一个严重的问题。

另一个坏处是生物多样性受到威胁。城市的快速扩张要占用大片土地来建设基础设施,比如机场、道路、购物中心和住宅小区。在这个过程中,许多动植物的栖息地遭到破坏,一些物种甚至可能从当地消失。

诚然,这一趋势对环境也有一个好处。城市居民通常住在较小的公寓里,可以依靠公共交通出行,所以他们人均消耗的能源和土地可能比农村居民更少。此外,城市污染带来的挑战促使科学家开发更环保的技术,比如电动公交车和节能建筑。

总之,我认为从农村向城市迁移对环境弊大于利。虽然城市生活可以更高效,也推动了绿色技术的发展,但严重的空气污染和生物多样性的丧失仍然是主要问题。因此,政府应该在公共交通和环保技术上投入更多,让城市发展更可持续。

Nowadays, a growing proportion of rural residents are leaving their homes for big cities. Personally, I believe that the drawbacks of this trend for the environment outweigh the benefits.

The key disadvantage is the worsening air quality in major cities. As more people move to and settle in these places, many of them are likely to buy private cars that rely heavily on fossil fuels. Burning these fuels releases large amounts of harmful gases, such as nitrogen dioxide, and as a result, air pollution becomes a serious problem.

Another downside is the threat to biodiversity. The rapid expansion of cities takes up large areas of land for infrastructure such as airports, roads, shopping centres and housing estates. During this process, the habitats of many plants and animals are destroyed, and some species may even disappear from the area.

Admittedly, this trend also has an environmental benefit. City dwellers usually live in smaller flats and can rely on public transport, so they may use less energy and land per person than people in the countryside. In addition, the challenges of urban pollution have encouraged scientists to develop greener technologies, such as electric buses and energy-efficient buildings.

In conclusion, I believe the move from the countryside to cities does more harm than good to the environment. Although city living can be more efficient and has encouraged green technology, serious air pollution and the loss of biodiversity remain major concerns. Therefore, governments should invest more in public transport and environmentally friendly technologies to make urban growth more sustainable.
