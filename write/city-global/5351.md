## 题目

Many different countries have most shops and products as the same. Some consider it a positive development, whereas others consider it negative. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 总体是积极的:对消费者和企业的好处,大于对文化多样性的损害。

## 中文(用来翻译)

随着全球化加强了国家之间的联系,现在在世界许多不同的地方都能找到同样的商店和产品。一些人认为这是积极的发展,另一些人则认为是消极的。我认为,虽然它在文化上有一些弊端,但好处更大。

一方面,这一现象有明显的好处。从消费者的角度看,能买到全球品牌让人们的生活更丰富,也提高了生活水平,因为这些产品往往已经在许多国家证明了自己成功、可靠。它们还帮助不同国家的人建立联系,因为大家用着同样的品牌、有着同样的体验。此外,经济上的好处也很显著。公司获得了更大的客户群,这能带来更高的收入。而且,标准化的商店和产品带来规模经济,降低了生产和配送成本,让生产者和消费者都省了钱。

另一方面,它的负面影响,尤其是对文化的影响,也很难忽视。一模一样的商店和产品到处扩张,会导致文化同质化。随着当地人接受外国的产品和生活方式,他们自己的传统和习俗可能变得不那么重要。例如,许多民族正逐渐放弃自己的传统服装,改穿 T 恤、牛仔裤和西装。此外,面对全球品牌的激烈竞争,本地企业可能难以生存,这进一步削弱了当地的特色。

总之,虽然统一的商店和产品到处扩张可能损害文化多样性,但我认为它给消费者和生产者带来的好处更重要。

As globalisation strengthens the connections between countries, the same shops and products can now be found in many different parts of the world. While some people see this as a positive development, others consider it negative. In my view, although it has some cultural drawbacks, its benefits are greater.

On the one hand, there are clear benefits to this phenomenon. From the consumer's perspective, access to global brands adds variety to people's lives and raises their standard of living, as these products have often proved successful and reliable in many countries. They also help people from different nations connect with each other, since they share the same brands and experiences. Additionally, the economic advantages are significant. Companies gain a larger customer base, which can lead to higher revenue. Furthermore, standardised shops and products bring economies of scale, reducing production and distribution costs, so that both producers and consumers save money.

On the other hand, the negative effects, particularly on culture, are hard to ignore. The spread of identical shops and products can lead to cultural homogenisation. As local people adopt foreign products and lifestyles, their own traditions and customs may become less important. For instance, many ethnic groups are gradually giving up their traditional clothing in favour of T-shirts, jeans and business suits. Moreover, facing intense competition from global brands, locally owned businesses may struggle to survive, which further weakens local identity.

In conclusion, although the spread of uniform shops and products may harm cultural diversity, I believe its benefits for consumers and producers are more significant.
