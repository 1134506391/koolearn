## 题目

Some people think that the best way to reduce the time spent traveling to work is to replace parks and gardens close to the city centre with apartment buildings for commuters, but others disagree. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 同意反方:这不是减少通勤的最好办法,因为房价太高,在家办公更可行,而且公园本身有价值。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,把市中心附近的公园和花园改建成公寓楼,是减少通勤时间最有效的办法,但也有人不同意。在这篇文章中,我将先分析两种观点,再给出我自己的看法。

支持这个想法的人认为,在市中心附近的公园和花园上建公寓,是帮助通勤者减少上班路上时间的实际办法。最明显的原因是,人们会住得离工作地点近得多,所以花在路上的时间就会减少。相比之下,即使有快速列车和地铁这样的快速交通工具,住在离市中心很远的工人也不太可能像住在附近的人那样快地到达。

然而,另一些人认为这不是最好的办法,理由有两个。第一,市中心附近的房子对大多数人来说太贵,买不起,所以许多工人仍然不得不住在远处便宜的地方。因此,问题还是解决不了。第二,还有更好的选择,比如允许 IT 和在线教育等行业的员工在家办公。这不仅让他们免去了通勤,还为那些仍需去办公室的人减少了交通拥堵。此外,公园和花园是城市居民放松和锻炼的宝贵绿地,一旦被建上房子,几乎不可能再恢复。

总之,双方观点都有道理。但总的来说,我认为把市中心附近的公园和花园改建成公寓并不是减少通勤时间的最好办法,因为大多数人买不起那里的房子,而在家办公是一个更容易实现的解决方案。

Some people argue that replacing parks and gardens near the city centre with apartment buildings is the most effective way to reduce commuting time, while others disagree. In this essay, I will analyse both views before giving my own opinion.

Supporters of this idea believe that building apartments on parks and gardens close to the city centre is a practical way to help commuters spend less time travelling to work. The most obvious reason is that people would live much closer to their workplaces, so they would spend less time on the road. In contrast, workers who live far from the city centre are unlikely to travel as quickly as those living nearby, even if fast transport such as express trains and the underground is available.

Others, however, argue that this is not the best solution, for two reasons. Firstly, housing near the city centre is too expensive for most people to buy, so many workers would still have to live in cheaper areas far away. Therefore, the problem would not be solved. Secondly, there are better alternatives, such as allowing employees in fields like IT and online education to work from home. This not only saves them from travelling but also reduces traffic congestion for those who still need to go to the office. Moreover, parks and gardens are valuable green spaces where city residents can relax and exercise, and once they are built over, they are almost impossible to replace.

In conclusion, both sides of the argument have merits. On balance, however, I believe that replacing parks and gardens near the city centre with apartments is not the best way to reduce commuting time, because most people could not afford homes there, and working from home is a more achievable solution.
