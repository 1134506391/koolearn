## 题目

Young people are leaving their homes from rural areas to study or work in the cities. What are the reasons? Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 原因是城市的教育资源和工作机会;弊大于利:年轻人个人受益,但城乡差距被拉大。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今,越来越多的年轻人离开农村去城市学习或工作。在这篇文章中,我将讨论这一趋势的原因,并解释为什么我认为这在很大程度上是一个负面的发展。

年轻人选择在城市读书,主要是因为那里有丰富的教育资源。和农村的图书馆相比,大城市的图书馆宽敞,藏书多得多,能帮助学习者更有效地获取知识。同时,设备齐全的实验室给理科学生提供了大量积累实践经验的机会。同样,学外语的学生有更多和外国人交流的机会,这能加快他们的学习。

去城市找工作的人主要认为,大城市能提供种类多得多的工作。随着城市快速发展,大量的工作岗位被创造出来,适合不同技能和教育水平的年轻人。因此,即使是没受过良好教育的人,至少也能找到低技能的工作,养活自己。

诚然,这种转移对年轻人自己有好处,他们获得了更好的教育和更高的收入。但我认为,它有一个更严重的弊端:城乡经济差距不断扩大。随着越来越多的年轻人涌入城市,城市不断发展,而他们的家乡只剩下以中老年人为主的居民,这些人的生产力太低,无法推动当地发展。例如,在中国的许多农村,因为没有足够的年轻人耕种,农田被撂荒。结果,城乡之间的经济失衡变得更加严重。

总之,更好的教育和更多的工作机会是年轻人去城市的两个主要原因。虽然他们个人受益,但我认为弊大于利,因为这一趋势拉大了城市和农村之间的差距。

Nowadays, an increasing number of young people are leaving rural areas for cities in order to study or work. In this essay, I will discuss the reasons for this trend and explain why I consider it a largely negative development.

Young people choose to study in urban areas mainly because of the abundant educational resources there. Compared with those in rural areas, libraries in big cities are spacious and hold far more books, which helps learners acquire knowledge more effectively. Meanwhile, well-equipped laboratories offer science students plenty of opportunities to gain hands-on experience. Similarly, students of foreign languages have more chances to communicate with foreigners, which speeds up their learning.

Those who look for work in cities mainly believe that big cities offer a much wider range of jobs. As cities develop rapidly, a large number of positions are created for young people with different skills and levels of education. Thus, even those who are not well educated can at least find low-skilled jobs and earn a living.

Admittedly, this shift benefits the young people themselves, who gain better education and higher incomes. However, in my opinion, it has a more serious drawback: the widening economic gap between urban and rural areas. As more young people pour into cities, cities keep growing, while their hometowns are left with mainly middle-aged and elderly residents, whose productivity is too low to drive local development. In many villages in China, for example, farmland has been abandoned because there are not enough young people to work on it. As a result, the economic imbalance between the two areas becomes worse.

In conclusion, better education and more job opportunities are the two main reasons why young people move to cities. Although they benefit personally, I believe the disadvantages outweigh the advantages, because the trend widens the gap between cities and the countryside.
