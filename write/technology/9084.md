## 题目

In modern society, it is possible to go shopping, work and communicate via the Internet without face-to-face contact with one another. To what extent do you think this is a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 积极为主:工作效率更高、购物选择更多;但要平衡线上和线下的交流。

## 中文(用来翻译)

数字技术的发展意味着人们现在可以在网上购物、工作和交流,而不需要面对面见面。我认为,这一趋势对人们生活的积极影响大于消极影响。

一方面,依赖互联网有一些明显的缺点。例如,网上购物的人无法检查产品的实际质量,买衣服和鞋子时这个问题尤其突出。这常常导致购买决定失误和失望。此外,主要在网上交流可能会削弱人际关系。当人们严重依赖技术来交流时,他们可能会与社会隔绝。即使是视频通话,也很难注意到对方的肢体语言和语气,这可能导致误解。

然而,网上互动带来的好处要多得多。一个重要的好处是工作效率更高。远程办公省去了每天的通勤和高峰期交通带来的压力。出现问题时,员工可以很快发起视频通话,而不用安排面对面的会议。此外,网购的人选择范围大得多,因为互联网让他们可以搜索来自世界各地的产品。Shopee、TikTok 和亚马逊等平台让人们在家里就能舒舒服服地浏览和比较商品。这大大提高了许多人的生活质量,特别是那些住在偏远地区或行动不便的人。

总之,虽然所有事情都在网上做带来了一些挑战,但我认为积极影响远远大于消极影响。不过,在虚拟交流上花太多时间会损害人际关系,所以人们应该平衡线上和线下的交流。

The development of digital technology means that people can now shop, work and communicate online without meeting each other face to face. In my view, this trend has a more positive influence on people's lives than a negative one.

On the one hand, relying on the internet has some notable downsides. For instance, people who shop online cannot check the actual quality of products, which is a particular problem when buying clothes and shoes. This often leads to poor purchasing decisions and disappointment. Furthermore, communicating mainly online may weaken human relationships. When people rely heavily on technology to communicate, they may become socially isolated. Even on video calls, it is harder to notice body language and tone of voice, which can lead to misunderstandings.

However, online interaction brings many more benefits. One significant benefit is greater work efficiency. Remote work removes the daily commute and the stress of rush-hour traffic. When problems arise, employees can quickly start a video call instead of arranging a face-to-face meeting. Additionally, online shoppers have a far wider choice, as the internet allows them to search for products from around the world. Platforms such as Shopee, TikTok and Amazon enable people to browse and compare products from the comfort of their homes. This has greatly improved the quality of life for many people, particularly those who live in remote areas or have difficulty moving around.

In conclusion, while doing everything online presents some challenges, I believe the positive effects far outweigh the negative ones. Nevertheless, spending too much time on virtual contact can damage relationships, so people should balance online and offline interaction.
