## 题目

Some people believe using modern technology (digital photographs, blogs) is the best way to record and remember important personal events. Others think the traditional method (making photo albums, writing diaries) is better. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 两种方式都有效,选哪种取决于个人喜好。

## 中文(用来翻译)

技术进步在许多方面彻底改变了我们的生活方式,包括我们保存珍贵经历的方式。虽然人们的偏好可能不同,但我认为,现代技术和传统方式哪个更好,并没有绝对正确的答案。

有些人支持用数码方式记录重要时刻,这是合理的,尤其是在各种电子设备的陪伴下长大的年轻一代。对他们来说,用手机拍照或记录自己的经历既自然又方便,而且随时随地都能查看。如果发到社交媒体上,这些内容还能轻松地和别人分享,让住在远方的亲戚朋友也能一起感受这些重要的时刻。此外,一部手机能存储成千上万张照片,所以几乎每个时刻都能被记录下来,帮助人们以后回忆。

另一方面,我们经常看到许多人仍然保持着做相册和写日记的习惯。人们更有可能细细品味一张纸质照片,而不是一张数码照片,因为它的触感和气味能唤起怀旧的感觉,而很少有人会经常在手机屏幕上翻看很久以前拍的照片。至于日记,虽然手写比打字需要更多的时间和精力,但写的过程能帮助人们更好地反思和整理自己的经历和想法,让这些重要的事件更有意义。

在我看来,这两种记录生活的方式都很有效,唯一的区别可能在于个人喜好。毕竟,更重要的是帮助我们留住那些美好的经历;只要能达到这个目的,用哪种方式都可以。

| 开头段 | Technology advance has revolutionized | Technological advances have revolutionised | 名词作定语要用形容词 technological;advance 可数,要用复数或加冠词 |

Technological advances have revolutionised our way of life in many respects, including how we keep our precious memories. While people may have different preferences, I believe there is no absolutely right answer to the question of whether modern technology or the traditional method is better.

It is reasonable for some people to support recording important moments digitally, especially the young generation, who have grown up surrounded by electronic devices. For them, it is natural and convenient to take photos or write about their experiences on their mobile phones, and these records can be viewed anytime and anywhere. They can also be shared easily on social media, allowing friends and relatives who live far away to enjoy these important events too. Besides, a single phone can store thousands of photos, so almost every moment can be captured to help people remember it later.

On the other hand, it is common to see many people who still keep the habit of making photo albums and writing diaries. People are more likely to treasure a printed photograph than a digital one, as its touch and smell can bring back a feeling of nostalgia, whereas few people often scroll through their phones to look at photos taken long ago. As for diaries, although handwriting requires more time and effort than typing, the writing process can help people reflect on and organise their experiences and thoughts, adding meaning to those important events.

In my opinion, these two ways of recording our lives are both effective, and the only difference may lie in personal preference. After all, what matters more is keeping those wonderful experiences; as long as this purpose is achieved, either method is fine.
