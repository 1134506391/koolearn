## 题目

Some people think that modern communication technology is having a negative effect on social relationships. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:总体是积极的(克服距离、危机中保持联系),问题可以通过合理使用来避免。

## 中文(用来翻译)

近几十年来,现代通讯技术发展迅速,改变了人们联系的方式。虽然有人认为它破坏了人际关系,但我认为它的总体影响在很大程度上是积极的。

现代通讯的一个主要优点是它能克服距离和时差。即时通讯和社交媒体让人们即使相隔很远,也能和家人朋友保持亲密的关系。例如,视频通话让住在不同国家的家人能看到彼此的脸,保持情感上的联系。此外,网络平台帮助人们结识兴趣相投的人,与其他国家的同事一起工作。而且,数字通讯在自然灾害或疫情这样的危机中起着至关重要的作用。例如,在新冠疫情封控期间,许多人依靠视频通话与无法探望的年长亲属保持联系。因此,现代技术在很多情况下并没有削弱社会关系,反而加强和扩大了这些关系。

然而,现代通讯技术也并非没有缺点。网上交流的便利有时会让人忽视现实中的关系。常常可以看到家人在餐桌上埋头看手机,而不是进行有意义的交谈。由于过度依赖数字通讯,一些年轻人也可能觉得面对面交流很困难。此外,社交媒体的算法往往只给人们看他们本来就认同的观点,这可能加深社会内部的分歧。

总之,虽然现代通讯技术带来了一些挑战,但它在加强人际关系和连接世界各地的人方面的好处远远大于它的坏处。为了充分利用它,人们应该有意识地使用,平衡线上和线下的交流。

In recent decades, modern communication technology has developed rapidly and transformed the way people connect. While some people believe that it has damaged social relationships, I believe that its overall effect is largely positive.

One key advantage of modern communication is its ability to overcome distance and time differences. Instant messaging and social media allow people to maintain close relationships with family and friends, even when they live far apart. For instance, video calls enable family members living in different countries to see each other's faces and stay emotionally connected. Besides, online platforms help people meet others with similar interests and work with colleagues across borders. Furthermore, digital communication plays a crucial role in crises such as natural disasters or pandemics. During the COVID-19 lockdowns, for example, many people relied on video calls to stay in touch with elderly relatives they could not visit. Thus, rather than weakening social bonds, modern technology has, in many cases, strengthened and expanded them.

However, modern communication technology is not without its drawbacks. The ease of online interaction sometimes leads people to neglect real-life relationships. It is common to see family members absorbed in their phones instead of having meaningful conversations at the dinner table. Because of overreliance on digital communication, some young people may also find face-to-face interaction difficult. Moreover, social media algorithms tend to show people only the views they already agree with, which may deepen divisions within society.

In conclusion, while modern communication technology presents some challenges, its benefits in strengthening relationships and connecting people around the world far outweigh its downsides. To make the most of it, people should use it mindfully and balance online and offline interaction.
