## 题目

Some people believe that cheap and fast transportation is essential for successful international business, while others argue that advanced technology is more important. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 两者都不可或缺,但在当今的全球经济中,技术的影响更大。

## 中文(用来翻译)

随着全球化不断深入,国际贸易和跨境合作变得越来越普遍。有人强调廉价、高效的交通运输的重要性,也有人认为技术进步的作用更大。我认为,虽然两者都不可或缺,但在当今的全球经济中,技术的影响更大。

一方面,廉价、快速的交通运输是国际商业的物质基础。高效的交通系统让货物和商务人士都能快速、可靠地进行长途运输和出行。例如,价格实惠的航空旅行和高铁网络让公司代表可以与海外合作伙伴面对面会谈,建立信任,更有效地谈判合同。当公司通过开设工厂、办公室或商店向海外扩张时,这种直接接触往往是必不可少的。此外,现代航运网络保证了产品在国家之间顺利运送,这对依赖实物贸易的行业至关重要。

另一方面,先进技术已经改变了国际商业。现代通讯工具让公司可以通过视频会议和实时数据共享,跨国即时合作,减少了频繁出国的需要。这一点在以数字服务为主的行业尤其明显,比如软件开发、金融和在线教育,在这些行业中交通运输的作用有限。此外,电子商务平台极大地扩大了进入全球市场的机会。通过亚马逊这样的平台,即使是小企业现在也能把产品卖给海外顾客。

总之,交通运输提供了让国际贸易运转的基础设施,而技术则提高了全球商业的效率、规模和连通性。虽然两者都很重要,但我认为技术已经成为当今国际商业发展的主要驱动力。

As globalisation continues to deepen, international trade and cross-border cooperation have become increasingly common. While some people emphasise the importance of affordable and efficient transportation, others argue that technological advancement plays a more significant role. In my opinion, although both factors are indispensable, technology has become more influential in today's global economy.

On the one hand, cheap and rapid transportation forms the physical foundation of international business. Efficient transport systems enable both goods and businesspeople to travel long distances quickly and reliably. For example, affordable air travel and high-speed rail networks allow company representatives to meet overseas partners face to face, build trust and negotiate contracts more effectively. Such direct contact is often essential when companies expand abroad by opening factories, offices or shops. In addition, modern shipping networks ensure the smooth delivery of products between countries, which is vital for industries that depend on physical trade.

On the other hand, advanced technology has transformed international business. Modern communication tools allow companies to work together instantly across countries through video conferencing and real-time data sharing, which reduces the need for frequent international travel. This is especially true for industries based on digital services, such as software development, finance and online education, where transportation plays a limited role. Furthermore, e-commerce platforms have greatly expanded access to global markets. Through platforms such as Amazon, even small businesses can now sell their products to customers overseas.

In conclusion, transportation provides the essential infrastructure that allows international trade to function, whereas technology increases the efficiency, scale and connectivity of global business. Although both are vital, I believe that technology has become the main driving force behind the growth of international business today.
