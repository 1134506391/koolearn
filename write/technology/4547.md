## 题目

It is better for society and individuals if driverless cars are widely used. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 基本同意:能利用通勤时间、减少交通事故;但技术还需改进,司机可能失业。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,如果无人驾驶汽车得到广泛使用,个人和社会都会受益。我基本同意这个观点,因为人们可以利用路上的时间,而且交通事故可能会减少。

我持这一观点的主要原因是,人们可以充分利用通勤时间。随着无人驾驶汽车背后的技术越来越成熟,这些车辆能自己在车流中安全行驶,让人们在去目的地的路上做自己想做的事。例如,如果前一天晚上没睡好,他们可以打个盹恢复精力。他们也可以准备马上要在重要会议上用的演示文稿。

我考虑的另一个重点是,交通事故很可能会减少。与人类司机相比,无人驾驶汽车会严格遵守交通规则,所以道路会更安全。换句话说,超速、疲劳驾驶、酒后驾驶和闯红灯等危险驾驶行为都会大大减少。因此,随着事故数量的下降,许多原本会在车祸中失去的生命可能会得到挽救。

不过,这项技术也有一些问题需要解决。无人驾驶汽车目前在恶劣天气或复杂的道路情况下仍然会出错。此外,许多出租车和卡车司机可能会失去工作,所以政府需要帮助他们找到新的工作。

总之,我基本赞同无人驾驶汽车的普及能给个人和社会带来重大好处的观点。毕竟,人们的时间能得到更好的利用,车祸也更不容易发生。因此,我建议在技术变得足够安全的同时,对无人驾驶汽车进行投资并广泛推广。

Some people argue that both individuals and society will benefit if driverless cars are widely used. I largely agree with this view, because people can make use of their travelling time, and there may be fewer road accidents.

The main reason for my view is that people can make full use of their commuting time. As the technology behind driverless cars becomes more mature, these vehicles can move safely through traffic by themselves, allowing people to do whatever they want on the way to their destinations. For instance, if they did not sleep well the night before, they can take a nap to refresh themselves. They can also work on presentation slides for an important meeting.

Another important point is that the number of traffic accidents is likely to fall. Compared with human drivers, self-driving cars will strictly follow traffic rules, so the roads will be safer. In other words, dangerous driving behaviour such as speeding, driving while tired, drink-driving and running red lights will be greatly reduced. Thus, with fewer accidents, many lives that would otherwise be lost in car crashes could be saved.

However, there are still some problems with this technology that need to be solved. Driverless cars can still make mistakes in bad weather or complicated road situations. In addition, many taxi and lorry drivers may lose their jobs, so governments will need to help them find new work.

In conclusion, I largely agree that the widespread use of driverless cars can bring significant benefits to both individuals and society. After all, people's time can be used better and car accidents are less likely to happen. Therefore, I suggest that self-driving cars should be invested in and widely promoted as the technology becomes safe enough.
