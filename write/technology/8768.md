## 题目

Some people say that the increasing use of computers and mobile phones for communication has had a negative effect on young people's reading and writing skills. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 完全同意:自动纠错让人不注意错误,网络用语影响正式写作,碎片化阅读影响读长文。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,年轻人大量使用电脑和手机交流,损害了他们的阅读和写作能力。我完全同意这个观点,因为这些设备替他们改正错误,助长了非正式的语言,还取代了阅读长文章的习惯。

我持这一观点的主要原因是,年轻人学不会注意自己的拼写和语法错误。当他们在设备上打字时,拼写检查和输入法联想功能会自动改正错误,所以他们很少注意到这些错误。结果,当他们必须手写时,比如在考试中,他们就会一再犯同样的错误。

另一个重要原因是,年轻人在短信和网上聊天中经常使用随意的表达,比如用 "CU" 表示 "see you",还有 "LOL" 和 "dunno"。虽然这些缩写让交流又快又方便,但年轻人会逐渐习惯它们。当他们需要写正式的文章或求职信时,可能就很难选出合适的词汇和句型。

阅读能力也受到了影响。大多数消息和社交媒体帖子都很短,所以年轻人大部分时间每次只读几行字。久而久之,他们中的许多人发现很难专心阅读小说或学术文章这样的长篇文字。

总之,我坚信用电脑和手机交流损害了年轻人的阅读和写作能力,因为它掩盖了他们的错误,助长了非正式语言,还削弱了他们阅读长文的能力。因此,年轻人在享受电子通讯便利的同时,也应该花时间读书、练习正式写作。

Some people believe that young people's reading and writing skills have been harmed by their heavy use of computers and mobile phones for communication. I completely agree with this view, because these devices correct their mistakes for them, encourage informal language and replace the habit of reading longer texts.

The main reason for my view is that young people do not learn to notice their own spelling and grammar mistakes. When they type on their devices, the spell-checker and predictive text automatically correct their errors, so they rarely pay attention to them. As a result, they keep making the same mistakes when they have to write by hand, for example in exams.

Another important reason is that young people frequently use casual expressions in text messages and online chats, such as "CU" for "see you", "LOL" and "dunno". Although these shortcuts make communication quick and convenient, young people gradually become used to them. When they are required to write formal essays or job applications, they may struggle to choose appropriate vocabulary and sentence structures.

Reading skills are also affected. Most messages and social media posts are very short, so young people spend much of their time reading only a few lines at a time. Over time, many of them find it difficult to concentrate on longer texts such as novels or academic articles.

In conclusion, I firmly believe that communicating through computers and mobile phones has damaged young people's reading and writing skills, because it hides their mistakes, encourages informal language and weakens their ability to read long texts. Therefore, while enjoying the convenience of electronic communication, young people should also spend time reading books and practising formal writing.
