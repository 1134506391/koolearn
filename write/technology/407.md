## 题目

More and more parents today allow their children to play on computers or tablets (e.g. iPad) because parents think children should learn technology skills. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 弊大于利:影响社交能力和学习成绩;好处是将来工作需要的技术技能。

## 中文(用来翻译)

许多家长认为孩子掌握技术技能很重要,因此越来越多的家长让孩子使用电子设备。在我看来,这种现象弊大于利。

家长为了让孩子学技术技能而鼓励他们玩电脑和平板,这对孩子社交能力的发展是有害的。电脑和平板上的各种程序对孩子非常有吸引力,这大大降低了他们出门和同伴一起参加各种活动的意愿。渐渐地,他们可能会变得害羞,不敢和别人交往,从而缺乏社交能力。

此外,接触电子设备会对孩子的学习成绩产生不良影响。孩子沉迷于电脑和平板上各种有趣的应用,可能会花更多时间玩这些设备,而不是写作业。久而久之,他们会很难理解学习内容,考试屡屡失败,落后于同学。

然而,掌握新技术的使用能力,在孩子长大后找工作和完成工作任务时确实是一项宝贵的技能。由于技术在工作中被广泛使用,雇主更喜欢会用 Word、Excel 和 PowerPoint 等办公软件的员工。同时,擅长使用这些软件的人工作效率也更高。

总之,我认为家长为了让孩子学技术技能而增加他们使用电子产品的频率,坏处多于好处。毕竟,孩子既得不到足够的机会提高社交能力,也无法专心学习、取得更好的成绩。不过,我承认家长的做法能让孩子获得技术技能,将来在工作中受益。因此,家长应该监督孩子的屏幕时间,让孩子在学习技术技能的同时不受负面影响。

Many parents believe that it is important for their children to learn technology skills, so an increasing number of them allow their children to use electronic devices. In my opinion, the drawbacks of this trend outweigh the benefits.

Encouraging children to play on computers and tablets in order to learn technology skills is harmful to the development of their social skills. The programmes on computers and tablets are so attractive to children that they considerably reduce their willingness to go out and take part in activities with their friends. Gradually, they may become shy and afraid to socialise with others, which results in a lack of social skills.

Additionally, using electronic devices can harm children's academic performance. Addicted to the many interesting apps on computers and tablets, children may spend more time playing with these devices instead of doing their homework. Over time, they will find it difficult to understand what they are learning, keep failing exams and fall behind their classmates.

However, it is true that the ability to use new technology is a valuable skill when children grow up and look for jobs and do their work. Since technology is widely used at work, employers prefer workers who know how to use office software such as Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Meanwhile, people who are skilled at using such programs work more efficiently.

In conclusion, I think that letting children use electronic devices more often to learn technology skills brings more disadvantages than advantages. After all, children neither have enough opportunities to improve their social skills nor can they focus on their studies to achieve better results. However, I admit that this practice helps children gain technology skills that will benefit them at work in the future. Therefore, parents should monitor their children's screen time so that they are not negatively affected while learning these skills.
