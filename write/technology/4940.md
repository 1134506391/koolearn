## 题目

Some people think that robots are very important to human's future development, while others think they are dangerous and have negative effects on society. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 机器人会带来失业和安全问题,但好处更大,对人类未来很重要。

## 中文(用来翻译)

我们的社会一直在经历许多技术变革,其中之一就是机器人产业的快速发展。虽然有些人担心可能出现的负面影响,但我认为机器人能为人类未来的发展带来巨大的好处。

一方面,考虑到工作岗位可能会消失,对机器人兴起的担忧是有道理的。机器人是多功能的机器,往往能精确地完成任务,而且不需要保险和带薪假期等福利,所以在某些职业中,它们看起来比人类更有竞争力。汽车行业的装配线已经表明,从人工转向机器可以带来更高的生产率。人们的另一个疑虑在于安全问题。在某些情况下,机器人无法灵活地处理紧急情况,只会按照程序运行,从而造成本来人类可以避免的损失。这样的事故不可避免地降低了人们对自动化的信任。

另一方面,我赞同那些对机器人有信心的人。使用机器人对员工有好处,因为机器人把工人从重复、枯燥的任务中解放出来,让他们有更多时间从事创造性的工作。对于拆除炸弹或太空探索这样的危险任务,机器人能挽救生命,完成人类做不到的事情。此外,在日常生活中,这项技术已经被用于医疗行业,协助外科手术,或者帮助残疾人和老年人过上更好的生活。

总之,和任何革命性的技术一样,机器人会带来巨大的变化,包括失业这样的负面变化。但它们也会催生人们从未想象过的新行业和新企业。因此,我相信机器人会在人类未来的进步中发挥至关重要的作用。

Our society has been undergoing numerous technological changes, one of which is the rapid growth of the robotics industry. While some people are concerned about the possible negative impacts, I would argue that robots can offer vast benefits for humans' future development.

On the one hand, the worry about the rise of robots is understandable, considering that many jobs could disappear. Since robots are versatile machines that often complete tasks with precision and require no benefits such as insurance and paid holidays, they seem more competitive than human beings in certain occupations. The assembly lines in the car industry have already shown that the shift from human labour to machines can lead to higher productivity. Another concern that people have is safety. In some situations, robots fail to handle an emergency flexibly and simply operate as they are programmed, causing damage that a human could have avoided. Such incidents inevitably reduce people's trust in automation.

On the other hand, I side with those who have confidence in robots. Using robots benefits employees, as they free workers from repetitive and boring tasks and give them more time for creative work. As for dangerous tasks such as defusing bombs or exploring space, robots save lives and go beyond human abilities, achieving what people cannot do. Additionally, in daily life, this technology has been adopted in the healthcare industry, where it assists in surgery or helps disabled and elderly people lead better lives.

In conclusion, as with any revolutionary technology, robots will bring dramatic changes, including negative ones such as job losses. However, they will also create new industries and businesses that people never imagined. Therefore, I am convinced that robots will play a crucial role in humans' future progress.
