## 题目

It was predicted that with the development of technology, people would have more free time in 21st century. To what extent do you think the prediction has come true? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 预测基本没有实现:技术提高了效率,但也增加了对人们时间的要求。

## 中文(用来翻译)

人们曾预测,21 世纪技术进步的好处之一是人们会有更多的空闲时间。虽然技术确实让许多工作实现了自动化,提高了生产效率,但我认为这并没有让大多数人拥有更多的休闲时间。

一方面,技术确实让我们能更快、更轻松地完成许多任务。例如,在农业中,机器的使用大大提高了效率,农民能用比以往更少的时间生产更多的粮食。同样,互联网和数字通讯工具让许多人可以在家办公,减少了通勤时间。在家里,洗衣机和洗碗机每周也节省了好几个小时的家务时间。这些进步无疑节省了时间,也让我们的效率更高。

另一方面,同样的进步也增加了对我们时间的要求。由于智能手机和互联网,无论身在何处、处于哪个时区,我们都随时在线。许多员工被要求在下班后回复邮件和消息,牺牲了自己的空闲时间。此外,机器在许多领域取代了人工,导致就业市场竞争激烈。结果,人们不得不花更多时间学习新技能来保住工作,有些人甚至不得不再做一份兼职才能维持生计。

总之,虽然技术无疑让我们更有效率,但它并没有给我们更多的休闲时间。相反,对我们时间的要求只是发生了变化,在这个联系越来越紧密、竞争越来越激烈的世界里,我们每个人都需要找到工作和休闲之间更好的平衡。

One of the predicted benefits of technological progress in the 21st century was that people would have more free time. While technology has certainly automated many tasks and increased productivity, I believe this has not translated into more leisure time for most people.

On the one hand, it is true that technology has allowed us to complete many tasks more quickly and easily. In agriculture, for example, the use of machines has dramatically increased efficiency, meaning that farmers can produce more food in less time than ever before. Similarly, the internet and digital communication tools have enabled many people to work from home, reducing the time they spend commuting. At home, washing machines and dishwashers have also saved hours of housework every week. These advances have undoubtedly saved time and made us more productive.

On the other hand, the same advances have also increased the demands on our time. Because of smartphones and the internet, we are always connected, regardless of location or time zone. Many employees are expected to answer emails and messages outside working hours, at the expense of their free time. Additionally, machines have replaced human workers in many areas, which has led to intense competition in the job market. As a result, people have to spend more time learning new skills to keep their jobs, and some even have to take on extra part-time work to make ends meet.

In conclusion, while technology has undoubtedly made us more efficient, it has not given us more leisure time. Instead, the demands on our time have simply changed, and it is up to each of us to find a better balance between work and leisure in this increasingly connected and competitive world.
