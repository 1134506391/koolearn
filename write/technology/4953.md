## 题目

In the past, people ate local food in season. Now it is possible to have any kind of food from all over the world at any time of the year. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 利大于弊:饮食更丰富、促进经济;环境代价不能只看运输里程。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今,人们的餐桌上摆满了丰富而有营养的食物,其中许多是从其他地区运来的。尽管长途运输会增加碳排放,但我认为进口食物的好处更重要。

这种做法最主要的好处是,人们一年四季都能享受更多样的饮食,而不只是有限的本地食物。例如,冬天人们不用再担心缺少蔬菜和水果,因为那些在寒冷天气里种不出来的大部分农产品可以从温暖的地区运来。持续获得重要的营养,并且能吃到各种反季节食物,对身心健康都有好处。

此外,跨国的食物运输也促进了经济发展。全球贸易帮助了许多国家的经济,特别是那些严重依赖农业的国家,因为它们可以把农产品大批量地出口到更大的市场。这为当地人创造了更多的外汇收入和工作机会。

另一方面,在世界各地运输食物确实会给环境带来后果。人们普遍认为,与本地种植的食物相比,长途运输的食物要消耗更多的化石燃料,碳足迹也更大,这会加剧全球变暖。然而,运输里程只是环境成本的一部分。对于英国这样气候不适合种某些作物的国家来说,进口食物实际上可能更环保。否则,他们就得用加热的塑料大棚来种植那些原本生长在炎热气候里的蔬菜,这需要更多的能源,还会产生塑料污染。

总之,由于环境成本不能只用碳排放来衡量,我认为让人们能吃到更多来自其他国家的食物利大于弊,无论对消费者的生活还是对国家的发展都是如此。

Nowadays, people's plates are piled high with plentiful and nutritious food, much of which is shipped from other regions. Despite the increased carbon emissions caused by long-distance transport, I believe the benefits of importing food are more significant.

The main advantage of this practice is that people can enjoy a more varied diet all year round, instead of a limited choice of local food. For instance, people no longer have to worry about a lack of fruit and vegetables in winter, as most produce that cannot be grown in cold weather can be shipped from warmer areas. A continuous supply of essential nutrients and the excitement of eating different off-season foods are good for both physical and mental well-being.

Additionally, transporting food across borders also contributes to economic progress. Global trade helps the economies of many countries, especially those that depend heavily on agriculture, since they can export their produce in bulk to a larger market. This creates more foreign income and more jobs for local people.

On the other hand, it is true that shipping food across the world has environmental consequences. It is commonly believed that, compared with locally grown food, food that travels long distances uses more fossil fuels and has a larger carbon footprint, which makes global warming worse. However, transport miles are only one part of the environmental cost. For countries such as the UK, whose climate is unsuitable for certain crops, importing food can actually be more environmentally friendly. Otherwise, they would have to use heated plastic greenhouses to grow vegetables that normally grow in hot climates, which requires more energy and produces plastic pollution.

In conclusion, since the environmental cost cannot be measured by carbon emissions alone, I believe that the greater availability of food from other countries brings more advantages than disadvantages, both for consumers' lives and for nations' development.
