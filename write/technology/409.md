## 题目

Some people claim that public museums and art galleries will not be needed because people can see historical objects and works of art by using a computer. Do you agree or disagree with this statement? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:亲眼看实物的体验、互动活动和对当地经济的贡献是电脑无法替代的。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,公共博物馆和美术馆已经没有必要了,因为人们上网就能看到所有的历史文物和艺术品。我完全不同意这个观点,因为参观实体的博物馆和美术馆能给人们更丰富的体验,同时这些场所也能促进当地经济的发展。

我认为即使人们能用电脑看到历史文物和艺术品,公共博物馆和美术馆也不会消失,主要原因是参观这些场所能带来网上浏览无法获得的体验。首先,亲眼看到实物和在屏幕上看图片是完全不同的。站在一幅巨大的油画或一件几千年前的文物面前,人们能感受到它真实的大小、颜色和细节。此外,许多博物馆现在安装了各种高科技设备,包括 VR 设备,参观者可以借此扮演某个历史时期的人物。同样,美术馆也会组织各种活动,比如画画和制作陶器,让人们获得互动的、有趣的学习体验。

此外,博物馆和美术馆可以成为一座城市的旅游景点,吸引游客前来,从而促进当地经济。例如,许多人去北京主要是为了参观故宫,去巴黎主要是为了参观卢浮宫,但他们也会游览城市的其他地方。因此,收入不仅来自博物馆和美术馆的门票,还来自游客的酒店住宿、品尝当地美食和购买纪念品。

总之,我强烈反对因为有了互联网,公共博物馆和美术馆就不再需要的观点。这是因为不去这些地方,人们就无法获得同样丰富的体验,而城市也会失去一种促进经济增长的方式。

Some people argue that public museums and art galleries are no longer necessary, because all historical objects and works of art can be viewed online. I completely disagree with this idea, because visiting real museums and galleries gives people a richer experience, and these places also help the local economy to develop.

The main reason why I do not believe that public museums and art galleries will disappear, even though people can see historical items and works of art on a computer, is that visiting them offers an experience that online viewing cannot provide. Firstly, seeing a real object is completely different from looking at a picture on a screen. Standing in front of a huge oil painting or an object made thousands of years ago, people can appreciate its true size, colours and details. Furthermore, many museums have now installed high-tech equipment, including VR headsets, with which visitors can play the role of people from a particular historical period. Similarly, art galleries organise various activities, such as painting and making pottery, giving people an interactive and enjoyable learning experience.

In addition, museums and art galleries can become tourist attractions that bring visitors to a city, which will boost the local economy. For example, many people visit Beijing mainly to see the Palace Museum, or Paris to see the Louvre, but they also tour other parts of the city. Thus, income comes not only from museum and gallery tickets, but also from visitors' hotel stays, meals and souvenirs.

In conclusion, I strongly disagree with the view that public museums and art galleries will not be needed because of the internet. Without visiting these places, people cannot enjoy the same rich experience, and cities would lose one way of promoting economic growth.
