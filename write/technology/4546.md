## 题目

Many people use social media every day to get in touch with other people and news events. Do you think the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 利大于弊:保持联系、及时了解新闻;弊端是影响健康和假新闻。

## 中文(用来翻译)

社交媒体越来越受欢迎,人们每天通过它和别人交流、获取新闻。我个人倾向于认为这样做利大于弊。

人们每天使用社交媒体的主要好处是,他们能和别人保持良好的关系。在其他城市或国家上大学的年轻人能和家人朋友保持联系,方便地和彼此分享生活。上班族也能轻松地和客户沟通,这最终有助于提高他们的工作表现。

另一个主要优点是,人们能及时了解国内乃至全世界正在发生的事情。过去,由于当时的技术不发达,公众要在事情发生一两天后才能听说。相比之下,如今电子设备上的新闻应用会自动给用户推送即时消息,让他们不用等待就能了解时事。

然而,每天使用社交媒体的缺点也不容忽视。最明显的是,人们的身体健康可能受到威胁。如果他们花太多时间在社交媒体上聊天和看新闻,就可能过着久坐不动的生活,缺乏适当的锻炼,最终很可能会出现眼睛疲劳、头痛和背痛。此外,社交媒体上的假新闻传播得很快,有些人可能会相信并分享不真实的信息。

总之,我仍然认为,人们在日常生活中用社交媒体和别人交流、获取新闻,积极影响多于消极影响。不过,为了减少弊端,我建议家长控制孩子使用屏幕的时间,成年人多参加各种活动,并且在分享新闻之前先核实。

Social media is becoming increasingly popular, and people use it every day to communicate with others and get the news. Personally, I tend to believe that the benefits of this outweigh the drawbacks.

The main benefit of using social media every day is that people can maintain good relationships with others. Young people attending university in another city or country can keep in touch with their family and friends and easily share their lives with each other. Working adults can also communicate with their clients easily, which eventually helps to improve their work performance.

Another major advantage is that people can find out what is happening across their country or around the world in good time. In the past, the public heard about events one or two days after they had happened, as the technology at that time was less developed. In contrast, news apps on electronic devices today automatically send users instant notifications, allowing them to learn about current affairs without waiting.

However, the disadvantages of using social media every day cannot be ignored. The most obvious one is that people's physical health may be harmed. If they spend too much time on social media chatting and following the news, they may lead an inactive lifestyle without proper exercise. In the end, they are likely to suffer from tired eyes, headaches and backaches. In addition, fake news spreads quickly on social media, and some people may believe and share information that is not true.

In conclusion, I still believe that using social media in everyday life to talk to people and get the news brings more positive effects than negative ones. However, to reduce the drawbacks, I suggest that parents should limit their children's screen time, adults should take part in various activities, and everyone should check the news before sharing it.
