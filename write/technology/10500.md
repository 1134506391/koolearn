## 题目

Although modern technology has made international communication very easy, many business people still travel long distances for business meetings. Do the advantages of face-to-face meetings outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 利大于弊:成本更高,但能减少误解、建立长期信任。

## 中文(用来翻译)

虽然视频会议、即时通讯和电子邮件等数字技术让国际交流容易了许多,但许多商务人士仍然选择出国开会。尽管成本更高,但我认为面对面的会议带来的好处更多。

不可否认,这样的会议需要公司和个人投入更多的时间、金钱和精力,这可能是一种负担。长途旅行需要额外的时间和体力,机票和酒店也增加了公司的开支。它还会影响员工的工作与生活平衡。相比之下,现代技术已经大大减少了地理障碍,让亲自到场变得没那么必要。

尽管有这个缺点,面对面的交流有无法替代的优势。第一是它能减少误解,尤其是文化差异造成的误解。当面会议结合了语言和非语言的交流,包括眼神交流、肢体语言和面部表情,这些都能帮助人们更准确地理解对方。

网络平台无法复制的另一个好处是建立牢固的商业关系。网上交流有时会让人觉得缺少人情味,因为没有机会通过闲聊或一起吃饭来建立情感联系。人们通常更容易信任见过面的人,并与他们合作,而不是只在网上认识的人。例如,买家参观供应商的工厂时,可以亲眼看到生产过程,这比视频通话更能建立信心。因此,长期协议更有可能在面对面时达成。

总之,尽管需要额外的时间和金钱,面对面的商务会议仍然必不可少,因为它们让沟通更有效,也让长期合作更有可能。

Although digital technology, such as video conferencing, instant messaging and email, has made international communication much easier, many business people still choose to travel abroad for meetings. Despite the higher costs involved, I believe that face-to-face meetings bring more benefits.

It is undeniable that such meetings require companies and individuals to invest more time, money and energy, which can be a burden. Long-distance travel takes extra time and physical effort, and flights and hotels increase a company's expenses. It can also affect employees' work-life balance. In contrast, modern technology has greatly reduced geographical barriers, making physical attendance less necessary.

Despite this drawback, face-to-face communication has advantages that cannot be replaced. The first is that it reduces misunderstandings, especially those caused by cultural differences. In-person meetings combine verbal and non-verbal communication, including eye contact, body language and facial expressions, all of which help people understand each other more accurately.

Another benefit that online platforms cannot replicate is building strong business relationships. Online communication can sometimes feel impersonal, because there is no chance to build an emotional connection through small talk or a shared dinner. People generally find it easier to trust and cooperate with those they have met in person than with someone they only know online. For example, a buyer who visits a supplier's factory can see the production process with their own eyes, which builds far more confidence than a video call. Therefore, long-term agreements are more likely to be reached face to face.

In conclusion, despite the extra time and money required, face-to-face business meetings remain essential, because they make communication more effective and make long-term cooperation more likely.
