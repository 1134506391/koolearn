## 题目

Many scientists believe that intelligent life forms may exist on other planets and messages should be sent into space to contact these life forms. Other scientists think this is a bad idea because these life forms could be dangerous. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 主动发送信息风险太大;人类应该只「听」,不主动暴露自己。

## 中文(用来翻译)

一些科学家认为,其他星球上存在高级的外星生命,我们应该尝试和它们交流;而另一些科学家反对这个想法,因为这些外星生物可能会对人类构成威胁。本文将先分析这两种观点,然后提出我的看法。

科学家认为有必要尝试联系外星生命,这是可以理解的。原因之一是,人类可能会从中获得帮助。如果外星人比我们先进得多,我们也许能学到他们的技术,来解决能源短缺和疾病等问题。此外,和外星人交流可能会帮助我们了解宇宙,了解一个文明怎样才能长期生存下去。

然而,主动联系外星生命也可能让人类面临危险。首先,先进的外星生物可能会把人类视为威胁或可以利用的资源,进而攻击地球。历史上有过类似的例子:欧洲人到达美洲时,当地的原住民遭受了战争和疾病,失去了自己的土地。如果外星人的技术远远超过我们,人类可能会处于同样的境地。其次,一旦信息发出去,就再也无法收回,我们也无法控制谁会收到它。

总之,我认为双方的观点都有道理。但总的来说,我认为主动向太空发送信息风险太大。科学家可以继续用望远镜收听来自太空的信号,但在我们对外星生命有更多了解之前,人类不应该暴露自己的位置。

Some scientists believe that advanced life forms exist on other planets and that we should try to communicate with them, while others oppose this idea because these creatures could pose a threat to human beings. This essay will first analyse both views and then give my own opinion.

It is understandable that some scientists consider it worthwhile to try to contact intelligent life. One reason is that humans might receive help with their own development. If aliens are much more advanced than us, we might learn from their technology to solve problems such as energy shortages and disease. Furthermore, communicating with aliens could help us understand the universe and how a civilisation can survive for a long time.

However, contacting aliens could also put humanity at risk. Firstly, advanced alien life forms may see humans as a threat or as a resource, and then attack the Earth. History offers a similar example: when Europeans arrived in the Americas, the native peoples suffered from wars and diseases and lost their land. If aliens have technology far beyond ours, humans could end up in the same position. Secondly, once a message is sent, it can never be taken back, and we cannot control who receives it.

In conclusion, I believe that both sides of the argument are reasonable. On balance, however, I think that actively sending messages into space is too risky. Scientists can continue to listen for signals from space with telescopes, but humans should not reveal their location until we know much more about alien life.
