## 题目

Some people think that public libraries are a place to find information and read books. Others believe that computers replace the function of libraries, so building libraries is a waste of money. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 虽然电脑能完成部分功能,但图书馆仍有必要,建图书馆不是浪费钱。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为图书馆是人们查找信息和读书的地方,也有人认为建图书馆是浪费钱,因为人们用电脑也能做同样的事情。本文将先分析这两种观点,然后提出我的看法:尽管电脑技术能实现图书馆的部分功能,但建图书馆仍然是有必要的。

有些人认为图书馆是人们获取信息和读书的地方,这是有道理的。图书馆里收藏着大量各种类型的读物,比如小说、杂志和学术论文。学生,无论是中小学生还是大学生,都可以查阅书籍来完成学习任务,上班族也可以参考与工作相关的资料。此外,图书馆是免费的,所以那些买不起书的人也能读到书。我们也经常看到不同年龄的人去图书馆读书放松。例如,假期里许多孩子会和同伴一起去图书馆看书。

另一些人则认为,电脑可以代替图书馆,所以建图书馆是浪费钱。出版商已经把许多书制作成电子版,上传到各种网络平台上,让人们更容易获得这些资料。因此,如果人们想查资料或读书,就不需要亲自坐在图书馆里。更方便的是,电子书可以下载并保存在智能手机和平板电脑等电子设备上,这样无论何时何地,人们都能查阅参考文献、阅读自己喜欢的小说。

总之,我认为双方的观点都有道理。但总的来说,我坚信保留图书馆是重要的,并不是浪费钱。毕竟,并不是每个人都能在网上找到或买得起所需要的资料,而图书馆还为人们提供了一个安静的读书和放松的地方。

Some people think that libraries are places where people can search for information and read, while others believe that building them wastes money, because people can do the same things on their computers. This essay will first analyse the two views before presenting my own opinion, which is that it is still necessary to build libraries, even though computers can perform some of their functions.

It is understandable that some people see libraries as places to find information and read books. Libraries store many different types of reading materials, such as novels, magazines and academic papers. Students, whether they are schoolchildren or university students, can consult books to complete their assignments, and employees can refer to materials related to their work. In addition, libraries are free, so people who cannot afford books can still read them. It is also common to see people of all ages going to libraries to read and relax. For example, during school holidays, many children read books with their friends in libraries.

Others, however, argue that building libraries wastes money, because computers can replace them. Publishers have produced many books in digital formats and uploaded them onto a range of online platforms, which makes these materials easier to access. Therefore, people do not need to sit in a library if they want to find information or read books. Even more conveniently, e-books can be downloaded and saved on electronic devices such as smartphones and tablets, so people can study references and read their favourite novels whenever and wherever they want.

In conclusion, I believe that both sides of the argument are reasonable. On balance, however, I firmly believe that keeping libraries is important and is not a waste of money. After all, not everyone can find or afford the materials they need online, and libraries also provide a quiet place to read and relax.
