## 题目

New technologies have changed the way children spend their free time. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 弊大于利:影响社交能力和身体健康;好处是学会电脑技能。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今孩子们度过空闲时间的方式和过去的孩子不同,这主要是新技术造成的。在我看来,这种现象弊大于利。

一个主要的缺点是,孩子的社交能力可能不容易得到培养。当孩子待在家里一动不动,比如看电视或者在电子设备上玩游戏时,他们就不太可能和别人交流,尤其是同龄人。这让他们无法和同伴沟通、扩大社交圈子,也无法锻炼自己的社交能力。

另一个缺点是,孩子的身体健康可能受到威胁。长时间盯着屏幕会导致眼睛疲劳,损害视力。例如,在中国,越来越多的小学生需要戴眼镜,许多医生认为这与看屏幕的时间过长有关。同时,长时间使用这些新技术也会损害孩子的体态,因为他们一直坐在屏幕前不动。

尽管如此,如果把空闲时间花在新技术上,孩子也能学到重要的技能,比如电脑技能,这对他们将来的学习和工作都有好处。经常使用电脑,他们会变得熟练,然后就能轻松地获取网上的知识和信息,帮助他们完成学习和工作中的任务。

总之,我仍然认为如今孩子度过空闲时间的方式坏处多于好处。虽然使用新技术能从小培养孩子的电脑技能,但它无法代替人与人之间的交流,同时还会对孩子的眼睛和身体造成不良影响。

The way children spend their free time these days is different from how children used to spend it in the past, mainly because of new technologies. In my opinion, the downsides of this change outweigh the benefits.

One major disadvantage is that children's social skills may not develop easily. When children stay in one place at home without moving, watching TV or playing games on electronic devices, they are unlikely to interact with others, especially children of their own age. This prevents them from communicating with their peers, widening their social circle and practising their social skills.

Another drawback is that children's physical health can be harmed. Staring at screens for long periods can cause eye strain and damage their eyesight. In China, for example, a growing number of primary school students need glasses, and many doctors link this to too much screen time. Meanwhile, using these technologies for too long can also be bad for children's posture, because they sit still in front of the screen.

Despite this, if children spend their free time on new technologies, they can learn important skills such as computer skills, which can benefit their future studies and work. When they use computers regularly, they become skilled at using them. Then they can easily find knowledge and information online, which helps them complete tasks at school and in the workplace.

In conclusion, I still believe that the way children spend their free time today has more drawbacks than benefits. Although new technologies can develop children's computer skills from a young age, they are no substitute for human interaction, and they can also harm children's eyes and bodies.
