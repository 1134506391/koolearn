## 题目

Students can easily access information online, so libraries are no longer necessary. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:图书馆提供可靠的免费资源和安静的学习空间,还是社区活动的场所。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,既然学生可以轻松地在网上找到信息,图书馆就不再有必要了。我完全不同意这个观点,因为图书馆提供可靠的资源和安静的学习空间,同时也是重要的社区场所。

首先,图书馆提供大量可靠的免费资源。虽然互联网上的信息很多,但其中很多是不准确或过时的,学生往往很难判断哪些来源值得信任。相比之下,图书馆的书籍和期刊都经过图书管理员的挑选,而图书管理员还能帮助学生找到他们需要的东西。此外,许多学术期刊和电子书在网上需要付费,但学生可以通过图书馆免费阅读。这对那些买不起这些资料的家庭的孩子来说尤其重要。

其次,图书馆为学习提供了一个安静的地方。在家里,学生常常被电视、家人或者智能手机通知分散注意力。在图书馆里,周围的人都在读书学习,这能帮助他们集中注意力,学习效率更高。例如,在中国,考试前许多大学生会一大早就去图书馆排队占座位。

最后,图书馆是社区活动的场所。许多图书馆为儿童举办故事会,为老年人开设电脑课,还组织读书俱乐部。这些活动让不同年龄的人聚在一起,帮助他们结交有共同兴趣的朋友,这是网上搜索做不到的。

总之,我强烈反对因为学生能上网查资料,图书馆就不再需要的观点。图书馆提供可靠的资源、安静的学习环境和社区活动,因此,政府应该继续资助并维护图书馆。

Some people believe that libraries are no longer necessary because students can easily find information online. I completely disagree with this view, as libraries provide reliable resources and quiet study spaces, and they also serve as important community centres.

Firstly, libraries offer a wide range of reliable resources free of charge. Although there is a great deal of information on the internet, much of it is inaccurate or out of date, and students often find it hard to judge which sources they can trust. In contrast, the books and journals in a library have been chosen by librarians, who can also help students find what they need. Furthermore, many academic journals and e-books must be paid for online, but students can read them for free through a library. This is especially important for children from families who cannot afford these materials.

Secondly, libraries provide a quiet place to study. At home, students are often distracted by the television, family members or notifications on their smartphones. In a library, they are surrounded by other people who are reading and studying, which helps them concentrate and work more efficiently. In China, for example, many university students queue early in the morning to get a seat in the library before exams.

Finally, libraries are places for community activities. Many libraries hold storytelling sessions for children, offer computer classes for older people and organise book clubs. These activities bring people of different ages together and help them make friends with shared interests, which online searching cannot do.

In conclusion, I strongly disagree with the view that libraries are no longer needed because students can find information online. Libraries offer reliable resources, a quiet learning environment and community activities, so governments should continue to fund and maintain them.
