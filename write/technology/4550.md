## 题目

Some people say that travelling abroad is necessary for learning about other countries, while others do not think so because TV and the Internet can give the same information. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 两种方式各有优点,选哪种取决于一个人有多少时间。

## 中文(用来翻译)

过去几十年的技术进步意味着,普通人现在更容易了解世界其他地方的气候、风景和旅游景点。有人认为出国旅行是了解其他国家的唯一途径,也有人认为电视和互联网就足够了。我认为两种方式都有价值,选择哪种主要取决于一个人有多少时间。

有人说电子媒体能满足他们了解异国他乡的需求,我能理解他们的观点。例如,熟悉科技的年轻人经常浏览和推荐带有高清照片和 360 度视频的网站和应用。对于老年人和行动不便的人来说,他们无法去很多想去的国家,这些视频和纪录片能让他们在家里就看到世界。

然而,也有人认为,不管技术多么神奇,它都无法代替亲身经历。旅行爱好者很可能觉得自己的旅程比看纪录片或旅行视频博客更难忘,因为旅途中有独特的味道、触感和气味。同样,孩子们也能从这种亲身体验当地风景和文化的经历中受益,这是任何教育视频都无法提供的。

就我个人而言,我认为这取决于一个人有多少时间。对于忙于工作或学习的人来说,经常安排一周的出国度假是不现实的,所以在假期以外的时间,精心制作的视频可能是最好的替代方式。然而,如果时间充裕,去一个新的国家、沉浸在那里的生活中会非常有收获。

总之,出国旅行和媒体资源各有优点,人们可以根据自己的情况选择合适的方式来了解其他国家。

Technological advances over recent decades have made it much easier for the average person to find out about the climate, landscape and tourist attractions in other parts of the world. Some people argue that travelling abroad is the only way to learn about other countries, while others believe that TV and the internet are enough. In my view, both have value, and the choice mainly depends on how much time a person has.

Some people say that electronic media can meet their need to learn about foreign places, and I can understand their point of view. For instance, young people who are good with technology often explore and recommend websites and apps with high-definition photos and 360-degree videos. For older people and those with limited mobility, who cannot visit as many countries as they would like, these videos and documentaries allow them to see the world from home.

Others, however, argue that despite the wonders of technology, it cannot replace real experience. Travel lovers are likely to find their own journeys more memorable than watching documentaries or travel vlogs, because of the unique tastes, touches and smells of each trip. Similarly, children can benefit from experiencing local landscapes and cultures in person, which no educational video can offer.

Personally, I think it depends on how much time people have. For those who are busy with work or study, it is not realistic to take frequent week-long holidays abroad, so well-made videos may be the best alternative outside the holiday season. However, if people have plenty of time, visiting a new country and becoming absorbed in local life can be very rewarding.

In conclusion, international travel and media resources each have their own advantages, and people can choose the way that suits their situation to learn about other countries.
