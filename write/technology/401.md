## 题目

Research shows that more and more business training and business meeting increasingly take place online. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 利大于弊:省钱、提高效率;弊端是缺少面对面交流、存在信息安全风险。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今,越来越多的公司在网上开会、组织培训。我个人倾向于认为,虽然可能会出现一些问题,但这样做的好处更多。

主要的好处是可以节省一大笔钱。大企业通常在世界各地设有分公司,并且定期从总部召开会议、提供培训。如果会议和培训在网上进行,经理和培训师就不必去每个分公司,这意味着可以省下差旅费。

另一个主要好处是工作效率可以大大提高。如果发生紧急情况,公司可以马上召开网上会议,立即给出解决方案。同时,参加培训的员工能马上回到工作岗位,不浪费任何时间。

当然,网上商务会议和员工培训也可能带来负面影响。第一,缺少面对面的交流。在网上,人们很难看清对方的肢体语言,所以讨论可能会不太有效,员工之间也更难建立信任。第二,商业安全可能成为一个问题。如果系统不安全,黑客可能会入侵并窃取重要信息,公司可能因此遭受巨大的损失。

总之,我认为网上会议和培训的普及利大于弊,因为这种做法成本低,还能提高工作效率。不过,商业机密在网上会议和培训中可能被窃取。考虑到这种情况,建议企业不断升级自己的通讯系统,以避免这种情况发生。

These days, a growing number of companies are holding meetings and organising training sessions online. Personally, I tend to believe that this practice has more benefits, although some problems may occur.

The major advantage is that a large amount of money can be saved. Large companies normally have branches in different places around the world, and they regularly hold meetings and provide training from their headquarters. If meetings and training take place online, managers and trainers do not need to travel to each branch, which means that travel expenses can be saved.

Another key benefit is that work efficiency can be improved considerably. If something urgent happens, an online meeting can be held immediately to find a solution. Meanwhile, employees who attend training sessions can return to their work straight away without wasting any time.

Admittedly, online business meetings and staff training may also have negative effects. Firstly, there is a lack of face-to-face interaction. Online, it is hard to see other people's body language, so discussions may be less effective and it is more difficult for colleagues to build trust. Secondly, business security can become a concern. If the system is not secure, hackers may break into it and steal important information. As a result, companies might suffer a great loss.

In conclusion, I believe that the popularity of online meetings and training brings more advantages than disadvantages, as this practice is cost-effective and improves work efficiency. However, trade secrets may be stolen during confidential meetings and training. For this reason, businesses should keep updating their communication systems to prevent this from happening.
