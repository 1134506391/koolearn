## 题目

Some think that printed books are not necessary in the digital era, because all writing can be stored electronically. Others think printed books still play an important role. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 大部分书会电子化,但纸质书对一部分人仍有价值,会保留少量。

## 中文(用来翻译)

数字技术彻底改变了我们的生活方式,包括我们阅读的方式。有人认为纸质书已经没有必要,也有人仍然很看重它们。我认为,大部分书最终都会电子化,但纸质书对某些人群来说仍会发挥作用。

电子书正在取代纸质书,原因有几个。第一是方便。有了电子阅读器或智能手机,人们无论在哪里都能阅读,不用带着沉重的书。此外,一台设备可以存储几百甚至几千本书,这比笨重的纸质书有明显的优势。环境因素也很重要,因为印刷纸质书需要大量纸张,而且许多卖不出去的书最终成了垃圾。因此,从生态的角度看,减少纸质书的数量是有道理的。

另一方面,许多人仍然更喜欢用传统的方式阅读。对他们来说,阅读可以是一种仪式:他们选一个安静的地方、一把舒服的椅子,享受纸张的触感、油墨的气味和慢节奏的阅读体验。除了这些热爱阅读的人,有些群体也会继续依赖纸质书,比如觉得数字设备难用的老年人,以及被建议限制看屏幕时间的孩子。对这些人来说,纸质书仍然值得保留。

总之,我认为随着技术的快速发展,大部分书面材料都会电子化,同时会保留少量纸质版本,供那些不愿意或无法用电子设备阅读的人使用。虽然这种转变需要时间,但走向数字阅读的趋势是无法阻挡的。

Digital technology has revolutionised the way we live, including how we read. While some people argue that printed books are no longer necessary, others still value them highly. In my view, most books will eventually be digitised, but printed books will continue to play a role for certain groups of people.

There are several reasons why electronic books are replacing printed ones. The first is convenience. With an e-reader or a smartphone, people can read wherever they are without carrying heavy books. Furthermore, a single device can store hundreds or even thousands of books, which is a clear advantage over bulky printed volumes. Environmental considerations also come into play, because producing printed books requires large amounts of paper, and many unsold copies end up as waste. From an ecological perspective, therefore, it makes sense to reduce the number of printed books.

On the other hand, many people still prefer to read in the traditional way. For them, reading can be a ritual: they choose a quiet place and a comfortable chair, and enjoy the feel of the paper, the smell of the ink and the slow pace of the experience. Besides these enthusiastic readers, some groups will continue to rely on printed books, such as elderly people, who may find digital devices difficult to use, and children, who are advised to limit their screen time. For these people, printed books are still worth keeping.

In conclusion, I believe that with the rapid advance of technology, most written material will be digitised, while a small number of printed copies will remain for people who are unwilling or unable to read digitally. Although this change will take time, the move towards digital reading cannot be stopped.
