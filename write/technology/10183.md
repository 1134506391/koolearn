## 题目

Some people think that technology brings mostly benefits to people's lives. Others think that technology brings mostly disadvantages. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 技术本身没有好坏,只要管理得当,好处多于坏处。

## 中文(用来翻译)

在当今世界,技术无处不在,它从根本上改变了我们的生活、工作和交流方式。有人认为技术主要带来好处,也有人认为它主要带来问题。我认为,只要管理得当,技术带来的好处多于坏处。

支持技术的人强调它的许多优点。在工作中,电脑和互联网大大提高了生产效率,过去需要几天才能完成的任务现在几分钟就能完成。在医疗方面,先进的诊断工具和在线医疗服务让人们更容易得到治疗,尤其是住在偏远地区的人。在日常生活中,智能手机让人们点几下就能付账单、购物和联系家人。这些发展显然提高了数百万人的生活质量,也让人们活得更长。

另一方面,批评者担心技术带来的问题。一个主要的担忧是隐私:科技公司收集了大量个人数据,这些数据可能被泄露或滥用。另一个担忧是就业。自动化和人工智能已经取代了许多工作,比如工厂工人和超市收银员,让一些人失业,也迫使另一些人为新职业重新接受培训。

我认为,技术本身没有好坏之分;它的影响取决于人们如何使用和监管它。例如,严格的数据保护法律可以保护人们的隐私,政府的培训项目可以帮助工人转到新的工作岗位。此外,技术的好处应该分配得更公平,这样较贫困的社区就不会被落下。

总之,虽然技术确实带来了一些问题,但我认为它的好处更大。通过谨慎地管理技术,社会可以在享受其优势的同时减少它的危害。

In today's world, technology is everywhere, and it has fundamentally changed how we live, work and communicate. While some people believe that technology mainly brings benefits, others argue that it mostly causes problems. In my view, technology brings more benefits than drawbacks, as long as it is properly managed.

Supporters of technology point to its many advantages. In the workplace, computers and the internet have greatly increased productivity, allowing tasks that once took days to be completed in minutes. In healthcare, advanced diagnostic tools and online medical services have improved access to treatment, especially for people in remote areas. In daily life, smartphones allow people to pay bills, shop and contact their families with a few taps. These developments have clearly improved the quality of life for millions of people and helped people live longer.

On the other hand, critics are concerned about the problems technology brings. One major worry is privacy: technology companies collect huge amounts of personal data, which can be leaked or misused. Another concern is employment. Automation and artificial intelligence have replaced many jobs, such as factory workers and supermarket cashiers, leaving some people without work and forcing others to retrain for new careers.

In my opinion, technology itself is neither good nor bad; its impact depends on how it is used and regulated. For example, strict data protection laws can protect people's privacy, and government training programmes can help workers move into new jobs. In addition, the benefits of technology should be shared more fairly, so that poorer communities are not left behind.

In conclusion, although technology does create some problems, I believe its benefits are greater. By managing it carefully, society can enjoy its advantages while reducing its harms.
