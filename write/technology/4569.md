## 题目

More and more people use computers and other electronic devices to access information. Therefore, there is no longer a need to use printed books, magazines, or newspapers. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 基本同意:电子信息更方便、更及时;但纸质读物对一部分人和一部分用途仍有价值。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,由于越来越多的人通过各种现代设备获取信息,纸质书、杂志和报纸已经没有存在的必要了。从方便和及时的角度来看,我基本同意这个观点。

我认为纸质信息正在变得多余,主要原因是它不如电子设备上的信息方便。以写研究论文的学生为例,如果用纸质资料,他们必须坐在图书馆里,一本一本地翻阅期刊,寻找可以引用的参考文献。相比之下,如果用电子设备,他们可以进入大得多的网络数据库,几秒钟之内就能搜出几百万条结果。

此外,印刷品无法提供最新的信息。报纸报道的至少是前一天的事情,因为印刷和配送到报亭需要时间。同样,许多参考书和杂志是几年前印的,所以里面的一些内容可能已经过时了。相比之下,网上的信息更新很频繁,因此人们可以获得最新的消息。

不过,纸质读物还不会完全消失。许多人仍然喜欢读纸质小说,因为它不伤眼睛,读起来也更放松。此外,一些老年人不习惯使用电子设备,还是更愿意读每天的报纸。

总之,我基本赞同随着网络信息的普及,人们不再那么需要纸质书、杂志和报纸的观点,因为网上找信息更容易,内容也更新。尽管如此,纸质读物可能仍会作为一种个人选择保留下来,特别是对于喜欢纸质阅读的人。

## 参考英文(Claude 修改版,不是原范文)

Some people argue that printed books, magazines and newspapers are no longer necessary because an increasing number of people can now get information through various modern devices. I largely agree with this idea in terms of convenience and timeliness.

The main reason why I believe printed information is becoming unnecessary is that it is less convenient than information on electronic devices. Students who are writing research papers, for example, have to sit in the library and read journals one by one to find references if they use printed sources. In contrast, if they use their electronic devices, they can search a much larger online database, where millions of results appear within seconds.

Moreover, printed materials cannot provide the latest information. Newspapers report events from at least the previous day because of the time needed to print them and deliver them to news kiosks. Similarly, many reference books and magazines were printed years ago, so some of their content may be out of date. In contrast, information online is updated frequently, so people can access the most recent news.

However, printed materials will not disappear completely. Many people still enjoy reading novels on paper, because it is easier on the eyes and more relaxing. In addition, some elderly people are not used to electronic devices and still prefer to read a daily newspaper.

In conclusion, I largely agree that people need printed books, magazines and newspapers less now that information is widely available on the internet, because finding information online is easier and the content is more up to date. Nevertheless, printed materials are likely to remain as a personal choice, especially for those who enjoy reading on paper.
