## 题目

Films and computer games containing violence are popular. Some people say they have negative effects on society and should be banned. Others say they are just harmless relaxation. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 有风险,但完全禁止太过分;应该用分级制度和家长监督来管理。

## 中文(用来翻译)

暴力电影和电子游戏的流行,引发了关于它们对社会影响的大量争论。有些人担心它们的负面影响,呼吁禁止它们;另一些人则认为它们是一种无害的娱乐形式。本文将先分析这两种观点,然后给出我自己的看法。

一方面,有人担心经常接触暴力媒体会让人对真实的暴力变得不那么敏感。一次又一次地观看或玩这类内容,可能会让攻击性行为看起来很正常,于是观众会更容易接受用暴力解决冲突。对于道德观念还在形成中的年轻人来说,这一点尤其令人担忧。孩子可能很难区分虚构和现实,可能会模仿他们看到的东西,认为暴力行为很勇敢或者很酷。结果,校园霸凌甚至青少年犯罪可能会增加。

另一方面,许多人认为暴力电影和游戏是一种缓解压力的方式,不太可能影响心理健康的成年人的行为。对于工作压力大的人来说,这类娱乐是一种便宜又简单的放松方式。此外,有了家长适当的引导和年龄限制,年轻人也能学会区分娱乐和可以接受的行为。另外,完全禁止会限制创作自由,损害娱乐行业。

在我看来,虽然暴力媒体有一定的风险,但完全禁止过于极端,也不现实。相反,政府应该实行更严格的年龄分级来保护孩子,家长也应该监督孩子看什么、玩什么。这样,成年人仍然可以享受自己选择的娱乐,而不会危害社会。

The popularity of violent films and video games has sparked considerable debate about their impact on society. While some people worry about their negative effects and call for a ban on them, others argue that they are a harmless form of entertainment. This essay will examine both views before giving my own opinion.

On the one hand, there is concern that regular exposure to violent media can make people less sensitive to real violence. Watching or playing such content again and again may make aggressive behaviour seem normal, so that viewers become more accepting of violence as a way to solve conflicts. This is particularly worrying for young people, whose moral values are still developing. Children may find it hard to separate fiction from reality and may copy what they see, believing that violent behaviour is brave or cool. As a result, there could be more school bullying or even youth crime.

On the other hand, many people argue that violent films and games are a way to relieve stress and are unlikely to affect the behaviour of mentally healthy adults. For people who are under pressure at work, such entertainment is a cheap and easy way to relax. Moreover, with proper guidance from parents and age limits, young people can also learn to tell the difference between entertainment and acceptable behaviour. In addition, a complete ban would limit creative freedom and harm the entertainment industry.

In my opinion, although violent media carries some risks, a complete ban would be excessive and impractical. Instead, governments should introduce stricter age ratings to protect children, and parents should monitor what their children watch and play. In this way, adults can still enjoy the entertainment they choose without harming society.
