## 题目

Newspapers have an enormous influence on people's ideas and opinions. Why is the case? Do you think it is a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

> 题目原文 Why is the case 有语法错误,正确的说法是 Why is this the case?

**立场(一句话):** 原因是读报是日常习惯、报纸被看作可靠的来源;这主要是消极的,因为会削弱批判性思维。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今,报纸对人们怎么想、相信什么有相当大的影响。在这篇文章中,我将先讨论这一现象的原因,然后解释为什么我认为这主要是一个消极的发展。

报纸对人们的想法影响如此之大,主要原因是许多人经常读报。对一些人来说,尤其是老年人,每天早上读报是一个由来已久的习惯。无论他们读的是纸质报纸还是网络版,每天读到的观点都会逐渐塑造他们自己的看法。

另一个原因是人们把报纸看作可靠的信息来源。在经济或国际关系这样复杂的问题上,普通人往往知之甚少,但他们知道记者和专家的工作就是调查和解释这些问题。因此,许多读者几乎毫不怀疑地接受这些专业人士写的东西。

诚然,当报纸报道准确时,这种影响可以是积极的,比如提醒公众注意健康风险或诈骗。然而,在我看来,如果人们的想法过于受报纸的影响,他们可能会停止批判性思考。总是跟着报纸走的读者可能不会形成自己的观点,也不会质疑一篇报道是否有偏见。由于报纸可能会受到老板或广告商的影响,这可能导致整个社区都接受片面的观点。

总之,报纸影响人们的想法,主要是因为读报是一种日常习惯,而且报纸被认为是可靠的。虽然这种影响可能是有用的,但我认为它主要是消极的,因为它会削弱人们独立思考的能力。因此,读者应该比较不同的来源,认真思考自己读到的内容。

Today, newspapers have a considerable influence on how people think and what they believe. In this essay, I will first discuss the causes of this phenomenon, and then explain why I believe it is mainly a negative development.

The main reason why newspapers influence people's ideas so strongly is that many people read them regularly. For some people, especially older people, reading a newspaper every morning is a long-established habit. Whether they read a printed paper or its online version, the opinions they read every day gradually shape their own views.

Another reason is that people see newspapers as a reliable source of information. On complicated issues such as the economy or international relations, ordinary people often know very little, but they know that journalists and experts are paid to investigate and explain these issues. Consequently, many readers accept what these professionals write almost without question.

Admittedly, this influence can be positive when newspapers report accurately, for example by warning the public about health risks or scams. However, in my opinion, if people's thinking is too heavily shaped by newspapers, they may stop thinking critically. Readers who always follow what they read may not form their own opinions or question whether a report is biased. Since newspapers can be influenced by their owners or advertisers, this can lead whole communities to accept one-sided views.

In conclusion, newspapers influence people's ideas mainly because reading them is a daily habit and because they are seen as reliable. Although this influence can be useful, I believe it is largely negative, because it can reduce people's ability to think for themselves. Readers should therefore compare different sources and think carefully about what they read.
