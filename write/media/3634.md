## 题目

More and more people no longer read newspapers or watch TV programmes to get news. They get news about the world through the Internet. Is this a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 积极:网上看新闻更方便,还能在评论区发表自己的看法。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今,越来越多的人通过互联网而不是报纸或电视来关注时事。我坚信这是一个积极的趋势,因为它能让人们更方便地获取信息,还给了他们表达自己观点的机会。

我持这一观点的主要原因是,网络新闻更容易获取。几乎每个人都有一台能上网的设备,比如智能手机或平板,用它们无论何时何地都能了解世界上正在发生的事情。因此,他们没有必要花钱去报亭买报纸,也不用在特定的时间回家打开电视。例如,上班族可以在午休时用手机看最新的新闻。

另一个重点是,读者可以分享自己对新闻事件的看法。大多数网络新闻文章下面都有评论区,人们被鼓励在那里写下自己对这篇报道的想法。这增加了人们的参与感,也让他们能看到别人怎样看待同一件事。相比之下,读报纸和看电视新闻的人很少有机会这样做。诚然,有些网上评论可能很粗鲁或者有误导性,但大多数新闻网站都有删除这类评论的规定。

总之,我认为人们在网上看新闻,而不是看传统的报纸或电视,是一个积极的发展。他们不仅能更方便地了解世界大事,还能通过表达自己的观点参与讨论。

These days, an increasing number of people follow current affairs on the internet rather than in newspapers or on television. I firmly believe that this is a positive trend, because it allows people to get information more conveniently and gives them opportunities to express their own opinions.

The main reason for my view is that online news is much easier to access. Almost everyone has an internet-connected device, such as a smartphone or a tablet, with which they can find out what is happening around the world regardless of time or place. Therefore, there is no need for them to spend money on newspapers at a kiosk or to go home and turn on the television at a particular time. For example, office workers can read the latest news on their phones during their lunch break.

Another important point is that readers can share their ideas about news events. Most online news articles have a comment section below them, where people are encouraged to write their own thoughts about the story. This increases people's sense of participation and allows them to see how others view the same event. In contrast, readers of newspapers and viewers of TV news programmes have few chances to do the same. Admittedly, some online comments can be rude or misleading, but most news websites have rules to remove such comments.

In conclusion, I believe it is a positive development that people read news online rather than in traditional newspapers or on television. Not only can they learn about world events more conveniently, but they can also take part in discussions by expressing their own views.
