## 题目

Anybody can use a mobile phone to answer work and personal calls at any time, 7 days a week. Does this development have more positive or negative effects on both individuals and society? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 消极影响更大:工作打扰私生活、开车接电话危险、忽视身边的人。

## 中文(用来翻译)

手机让人们可以随时接电话。人们在享受这种便利的同时,也越来越意识到它对个人和社会的有害影响。在我看来,负面影响大于正面影响。

不可否认,随时都能联系到人,提高了个人和社会的效率。员工的工作效率可以更高,因为和同事的沟通不再受距离或时间的限制。同样,和家人朋友保持联系变得非常容易,亲人只有一个电话的距离。因此,整个社会都从更快的沟通和经济增长中受益。

然而,这种「永远在线」文化的弊端越来越明显。晚上和周末的工作电话打扰了人们的私生活,模糊了工作和个人时间之间的界限。结果,许多上班族发现很难实现健康的工作与生活平衡,这可能导致压力和疲惫。另一个危险在于公共安全。许多人觉得自己必须马上接每一个电话,即使是在开车的时候,这大大增加了交通事故的风险。

对社会来说,这一趋势也可能带来长期的负面影响。当人们花更多时间和远方的人通话时,他们可能会忽视身边的人。例如,我们经常看到一家人在餐桌上忙着看手机,而不是彼此交谈。久而久之,这可能会削弱面对面的关系。

总之,虽然能随时接电话的手机带来了一些好处,但我认为它的弊端对个人和社会来说都更严重。

Mobile phones have made it possible for people to answer calls at any time. While people enjoy the benefits of this, they are also becoming aware of its harmful effects on individuals and society. In my opinion, the negative effects outweigh the positive ones.

Undeniably, being reachable at all times improves efficiency for both individuals and society. Workers can be more productive, because communication with colleagues is no longer limited by distance or time. Likewise, staying in touch with family and friends has become so easy that loved ones are only a phone call away. As a result, society as a whole benefits from faster communication and economic growth.

However, the drawbacks of this "always-on" culture are becoming more obvious. Work calls in the evening and at weekends disturb people's private lives and blur the line between work and personal time. As a result, many working people find it very difficult to achieve a healthy work-life balance, which can lead to stress and exhaustion. Another danger lies in public safety. Many people feel they must answer every call immediately, even when they are driving, which greatly increases the risk of traffic accidents.

For society, this trend may also have negative long-term effects. When people spend more time talking to those who are far away, they may pay less attention to the people around them. For example, it is common to see family members at the dinner table busy with their phones instead of talking to each other. Over time, this may weaken face-to-face relationships.

In conclusion, while mobile phones that allow us to answer calls at any time bring some benefits, I believe the drawbacks are more significant for both individuals and society.
