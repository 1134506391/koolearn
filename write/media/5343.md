## 题目

With the development of communication technology, such as smartphones, tablets, and other mobile devices, some people think the disadvantages outweigh its advantages. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 基本不同意:能维系感情、提高工作效率;缺点是工作和生活的界限变模糊。

## 中文(用来翻译)

许多人用智能手机和平板等高科技设备和别人保持联系。然而,有人认为这种技术带来的坏处多于好处。我基本不同意这个观点,因为通讯技术能帮助人们维系关系、提高工作效率,尽管它会让人更难平衡工作和个人生活。

我持这一观点的主要原因是,移动设备能加强人与人之间的联系。许多人搬到其他城市或国家工作或学习,所以不能经常见到家人和朋友。有了智能手机,他们可以和亲人视频通话,分享自己的经历,不受时间和距离的限制。

另一个原因是,通讯设备让人们的工作效率更高。一方面,经理们即使在出差,也能用手机和员工讨论紧急的事情。另一方面,员工即使在公共交通上,也能及时回复客户的邮件。

然而,这种技术确实可能模糊工作和私生活之间的界限。虽然许多员工正式的工作时间是朝九晚五,但他们常常被要求在下班后回复消息和邮件。结果,他们失去了本该用来放松或陪伴家人的个人时间,比如一起吃晚饭或者辅导孩子写作业。

总之,我不同意通讯技术的发展弊大于利,因为它能帮助人们保持良好的关系、更高效地工作。虽然它可能让工作和生活的平衡变得更难,但如果人们给回复工作消息的时间设定明确的界限,这个问题是可以解决的。

Many people use high-tech devices such as smartphones and tablets to keep in touch with others. However, some people believe that this technology brings more disadvantages than advantages. I mostly disagree with this idea, because communication technology helps people maintain relationships and makes their work more efficient, although it can make it harder to balance work and personal life.

The main reason for my view is that mobile devices can strengthen the bonds between people. Many people have moved to other cities or countries to work or study, so they cannot see their family and friends easily. A smartphone allows them to make video calls to their loved ones and share their experiences, regardless of time and distance.

Another reason is that communication devices make people's work more efficient. For one thing, managers can discuss urgent matters with their staff on their phones even when they are on business trips. For another, employees can reply to their clients' emails quickly, even when they are on public transport.

However, it is also true that this technology may blur the line between work and private life. Although many employees officially work from nine to five, they are often expected to answer messages and emails after work. As a result, they lose personal time that should be spent relaxing or with their family, such as having dinner together or helping their children with homework.

In conclusion, I do not agree that the development of communication technology brings more disadvantages than advantages, because it helps people maintain good relationships and work more efficiently. Although it may make work-life balance more difficult, this problem can be solved if people set clear limits on when they reply to work messages.
