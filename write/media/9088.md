## 题目

In some countries, celebrities complain about reports of their private lives. Some people say that they should accept it as part of their fame. Do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 同意:名人靠公众关注成功,很多分享是主动的;但极端的跟踪偷拍应该受法律约束。

## 中文(用来翻译)

名人享受着名气和公众的关注,但当媒体报道他们的私生活时,他们却常常抱怨。有人认为这是出名的自然代价,我同意这个观点。

电影明星和歌手等公众人物的成功和商业价值,都来自公众对他们的关注。他们通过参加活动、代言奢侈品牌、发布新歌或新电影,主动寻求曝光。公众的关注正是推动他们事业的动力。此外,媒体报道常常能提升他们的声誉,比如展示他们时尚品味或慈善工作的报道。事实上,许多名人为了保持人气,会主动在脱口秀或 Instagram、微博等社交媒体上分享私生活的细节。这说明,他们私生活的很多分享是他们自己的选择,是和粉丝建立联系的一种方式。

诚然,没有人喜欢一直被盯着,有些摄影师和狂热的粉丝会以有害的方式侵犯名人的隐私,比如跟踪他们回家。这种极端行为应该受到法律的约束。然而,名人最常抱怨的往往是负面报道,比如关于吸毒、公开争吵或其他可能损害他们形象的不良行为的报道。名人一边从公众对他们生活的兴趣中获益,一边又只希望得到正面报道,这是不合理的。

总之,虽然名人和普通人一样拥有隐私权,但他们必须接受,公众的关注是出名不可避免的一部分。他们享受的特权是有代价的,而接受关于自己生活的报道就是这个代价的一部分。

Celebrities enjoy fame and public attention, yet they often complain when the media report on their private lives. Some people believe this is a natural part of being famous, and I agree with this view.

Public figures such as film stars and singers owe their success and commercial value to the attention they receive from the public. They actively seek the spotlight by attending events, promoting luxury brands and releasing new songs or films. Public attention is exactly what drives their careers. Moreover, media coverage often improves their reputation, for example through reports that show their fashion sense or their charity work. In fact, many celebrities willingly share details of their personal lives on talk shows or on social media platforms such as Instagram and Weibo to stay popular. This shows that much of the sharing of their private lives is their own choice, as a way to connect with fans.

Admittedly, nobody enjoys being watched all the time, and some photographers and obsessive fans invade celebrities' privacy in harmful ways, such as following them home. Such extreme behaviour should be controlled by law. However, what celebrities complain about most is often negative reporting, such as stories about drug use, public arguments or other bad behaviour that could damage their image. It is unreasonable for celebrities to expect only positive coverage while benefiting from public interest in their lives.

In conclusion, although celebrities have the same right to privacy as ordinary people, they must accept that public attention is an unavoidable part of fame. The privileges they enjoy come at a price, and accepting reports about their lives is part of that price.
