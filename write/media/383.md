## 题目

Nowadays, children spend a lot of time watching TV and playing computer games. Some people believe that this has negative effects on children's mental abilities. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 部分同意:看太多电视有害,因为孩子被动接收;适度玩游戏反而能锻炼思维。

## 中文(用来翻译)

如今,有人声称花大量时间看电视和玩电脑游戏会损害孩子的智力。我部分同意这个观点:我认为看太多电视会损害孩子的智力,但玩电脑游戏实际上能培养其中的一些能力。

我认为看大量电视会损害孩子智力的原因是,看电视不需要他们主动思考。看电视的时候,孩子是被动的观众。换句话说,他们只是接收节目提供的信息,而不是用大脑去处理和分析这些信息。久而久之,这可能会削弱他们的专注力和学习能力。

另一方面,玩电脑游戏能让孩子的大脑得到真正的锻炼。为了赢得游戏,孩子需要遵守规则;遇到困难时,他们必须仔细思考,然后才能找到有效的解决办法。许多游戏还需要良好的手眼协调能力。例如,在俄罗斯方块这样的益智游戏中,玩家必须一边提前计划,一边快速反应。因此,游戏能训练一系列思维能力,比如解决问题的能力和手眼协调能力,这些在学校里不一定能学到。不过,孩子玩游戏仍然应该限制时间,因为玩得太多可能会上瘾。

总之,我认为花大量时间看电视对孩子的智力有害,因为他们没有主动参与思考。然而,适度地玩电脑游戏能帮助培养许多思维能力。

These days, some people claim that spending a lot of time watching TV and playing computer games damages children's mental abilities. I partly agree with this idea: I believe that watching too much television can harm children's mental abilities, but playing computer games can actually develop some of them.

The reason why I believe that watching a lot of TV can harm children's mental abilities is that it does not require them to think actively. When watching TV, children are passive viewers. In other words, they simply receive the messages that programmes provide, instead of using their brains to process and analyse information. Over time, this may weaken their concentration and their ability to learn.

On the other hand, playing computer games gives children's brains a real workout. To win a game, children need to follow instructions, and when they face difficulties, they have to think carefully before finding effective solutions. Many games also require good hand-eye coordination. For example, in puzzle games such as Tetris, players must plan ahead and react quickly at the same time. Therefore, games can train a range of mental skills, such as problem-solving and hand-eye coordination, which are not always taught at school. However, children should still play for a limited time, as too much gaming can become addictive.

In conclusion, I believe that spending a lot of time watching TV is harmful to children's mental abilities, because they do not take an active part in thinking. However, playing computer games in moderation can help develop many mental skills.
