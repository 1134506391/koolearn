## 题目

Some people think that the news has no connection to their lives, so reading newspapers and watching television news programmes is a waste of time. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:国际新闻会影响物价和出行,看新闻还能培养思考能力。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,读报纸或看电视新闻是浪费时间,因为这些信息和他们的日常生活几乎没有关系。我不同意这个观点。

诚然,大量的新闻报道似乎和个人的日常生活无关。许多报道关注的是国际冲突和战争等全球性问题,这些离普通人的日常生活很遥远。大多数市民更关心自己社区的安全、上班路上的交通和当地学校的质量。从这个角度看,读这样的新闻似乎毫无意义。

然而,在当今全球化的世界里,新闻对个人生活有着实实在在的影响,尤其是在日常开支和出行计划方面。虽然全球事件看起来和个人无关,但它们常常会产生深远的影响。例如,中东的冲突可能会推高油价,从而提高许多产品和服务的价格,包括衣服和公共交通。同样,关注新闻的人知道哪些国家去旅游不安全,可以相应地改变度假计划。

此外,关注新闻是培养思考能力和国际视野的有效方式。当人们了解全球事件时,他们可以分析事件的原因和影响,进行批判性思考,并预测政治或科技未来的趋势。即使这些能力在短期内对工作或学习似乎影响不大,它们也能帮助人们提升自己,在职业上更有竞争力。

总之,虽然有些新闻报道在短期内似乎没什么用,但它们仍然影响着每个人的生活,从物价到出行都是如此,还能帮助人们培养思考能力。因此,关注新闻并不是浪费时间。

Some people argue that reading newspapers or watching TV news is a waste of time, because the information has almost nothing to do with their everyday lives. I disagree with this view.

Admittedly, a large amount of news coverage seems irrelevant to individuals' daily lives. Many reports focus on global issues such as international conflicts and wars, which are far removed from ordinary people's routines. Most citizens care more about the safety of their neighbourhood, traffic on their way to work and the quality of local schools. In this respect, reading such news may seem pointless.

However, in today's globalised world, news has a real effect on personal life, especially on daily expenses and travel plans. Although global events seem unconnected with individuals, they often have far-reaching effects. For example, conflicts in the Middle East can push up oil prices, which raises the cost of many products and services, including clothes and public transport. Similarly, people who follow the news know which countries are unsafe to visit and can change their holiday plans accordingly.

Furthermore, following the news is an effective way to develop thinking skills and an international outlook. When people learn about global events, they can analyse their causes and effects, think critically and predict future trends in politics or technology. Even if these abilities seem to have little effect on work or study in the short term, they help people improve themselves and become more competitive in their careers.

In conclusion, even though some news reports may not seem useful in the short term, they still affect everyone's life, from prices to travel, and help people develop their thinking. Therefore, following the news is not a waste of time.
