## 题目

The only purpose of advertising is to encourage people to buy more. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:卖东西是主要目的,但广告还能提供信息、建立品牌、传播公益信息。

## 中文(用来翻译)

人们对广告的一个常见批评是,它唯一的目的就是说服人们买更多东西。虽然这种说法有一定道理,但我不同意卖东西是广告唯一的作用。

必须承认,鼓励消费是大多数广告的主要目的。电视广告和网络推广都经过精心设计,通过激发人们对身份地位的渴望和害怕错过的心理,来制造购买欲望、促使人们冲动消费。此外,整个广告行业都是由想要提高销量的公司出钱支撑的。从这个角度看,很难否认广告主要是一种销售工具,因为几乎每一则广告最后都是在邀请人们购买。

然而,把这说成是「唯一」的目的,就过于简单化了。第一,广告能提供信息。它向公众介绍新产品,帮助消费者比较不同的选择,让他们做出更明智的决定。第二,广告不只是为了让人马上购买,它也被用来建立品牌形象和长期的忠诚度。公司在宣传上花费大量预算,展示质量和环保责任等价值观,希望和顾客建立情感上的联系。最后,有些广告和购买完全无关。公益广告,比如禁烟宣传和交通安全宣传,目的是改变人们的行为、保护他们的健康。

总之,虽然提高销量无疑是大多数广告的主要目的,但它远远不是唯一的目的。因为广告还能向公众提供信息、在品牌和顾客之间建立长期关系、传播重要的社会信息,所以我不同意广告只是为了鼓励人们多买东西这种狭隘的观点。

A common criticism of advertising is that its only purpose is to persuade people to buy more. While there is some truth in this claim, I disagree that selling is the only function of advertising.

It must be admitted that encouraging consumption is the main aim of most advertising. Commercials and online campaigns are carefully designed to create desire and encourage impulse buying by appealing to emotions such as the wish for status and the fear of missing out. Moreover, the whole industry is funded by companies that want higher sales. In this respect, it is hard to deny that advertising is mainly a tool for selling, since almost every advertisement ends with an invitation to buy.

Nevertheless, describing this as the "only" purpose is an oversimplification. Firstly, advertising provides information. It introduces new products to the public and helps consumers compare options, so that they can make more sensible decisions. Secondly, advertising is not only about immediate purchases; it is also used to build a brand image and long-term loyalty. Companies spend large budgets on campaigns that show values such as quality and environmental responsibility, hoping to build an emotional connection with customers. Finally, some advertisements have nothing to do with buying at all. Public service advertisements, such as anti-smoking campaigns and road safety messages, aim to change people's behaviour and protect their health.

In conclusion, while increasing sales is undoubtedly the main aim of most advertising, it is far from its only purpose. Because advertising also informs the public, builds long-term relationships between brands and customers, and spreads important social messages, I reject the narrow view that it exists only to encourage people to buy more.
