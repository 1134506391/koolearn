## 题目

Some people say that advertising has positive economic effects. Others think it has negative social effects because it will make people feel dissatisfied with who they are and what they have. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 两种影响都真实存在,但广告需要监管,以减少它的社会代价。

## 中文(用来翻译)

广告牌、传单和电视广告在日常生活中无处不在,不断地向人们展示最新的产品和服务。有些人称赞广告带来的经济好处,另一些人则认为广告让人们对自己拥有的东西不满意,从而危害社会。在我看来,这两种影响都是真实存在的,但广告需要受到监管,以减少它的社会代价。

有人认为广告对经济至关重要,我能理解他们的观点。如果没有反复的广告宣传,即使是最有创意的产品也可能无法接触到顾客,无法取得好的销量。对于新企业和小企业来说尤其如此,它们需要靠广告和知名品牌竞争。此外,广告行业本身也创造了大量的工作岗位,从平面设计师到文案和视频制作人员。

然而,另一些人批评广告让人们觉得自己不够好,我在一定程度上同意他们的看法。看完一则制作精美的广告后,因为负担不起屏幕上展示的生活方式而感到焦虑,这是很常见的经历。许多广告把完美的身材、昂贵的汽车和豪华的假期展示得好像很平常,这鼓励人们和别人比较。这种不断的比较会让人们对自己的外表和财产感到不满,有些人甚至会为了买负担不起的东西而欠债。

总之,虽然广告带来了明显的经济好处,但它对人们心理健康的负面影响不应被忽视。因此,政府、生产商和广告公司应该共同努力,比如为面向年轻人的广告制定规则,这样既能保留经济上的好处,又能减少社会代价。

Billboards, leaflets and TV commercials are everywhere in daily life, constantly showing people the latest products and services. While some people praise advertising for its economic benefits, others argue that it harms society by making people unhappy with what they have. In my view, both effects are real, but advertising needs to be regulated to reduce its social costs.

Some people argue that advertising is essential to the economy, and I can understand their point of view. Without repeated advertising, even the most innovative products may fail to reach customers and achieve good sales. This is especially true for new and small businesses, which need advertising to compete with well-known brands. In addition, the advertising industry itself creates a large number of jobs, from graphic designers to copywriters and video producers.

Others, however, criticise advertising for making people feel inadequate, and I agree with them to some extent. It is a common experience to watch a beautifully made advertisement and then feel anxious about not being able to afford the lifestyle shown on screen. Many adverts present perfect bodies, expensive cars and luxury holidays as normal, which encourages people to compare themselves with others. This constant comparison can make people feel dissatisfied with their appearance and possessions, and some may even get into debt to buy things they cannot afford.

In conclusion, although advertising brings clear economic benefits, its negative effects on people's mental well-being should not be ignored. Therefore, governments, manufacturers and advertising agencies should work together, for example by setting rules for advertisements aimed at young people, so that the economic benefits can be kept while the social costs are reduced.
