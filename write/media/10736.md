## 题目

Research indicates that nowadays some consumers are much less influenced by advertising than in the past. What do you think are the reasons for this? Do you think this is a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 原因是人们看清了广告、更相信网上的评价;这是积极的发展,省钱、环保、逼企业提高质量。

## 中文(用来翻译)

与过去相比,广告现在对许多消费者的影响小得多了。这一变化反映了人们理解广告和做购买决定的方式,我认为这是一个积极的发展。

广告的影响变小,主要原因是许多消费者现在意识到,广告唯一的目的就是卖产品。他们也明白,为了诱惑人们购买,广告里的商品常常被弄得看起来比实际更好。有了这种认识,他们自然就不太关注广告了。此外,社交媒体已经成为人们分享产品和服务使用体验的平台,许多消费者现在买东西之前都会先搜索这些信息。例如,在买新手机之前,很多人会去亚马逊或小红书上看用户评价,而不是相信广告。人们往往认为这些评价比广告更可靠。

我认为这一发展对消费者和社会都有好处。对消费者来说,它能省下原本会花在不真正需要的东西上的钱。例如,不理会广告的人,在旧手机还很好用的时候,不会被说服去买最新款的智能手机或厨房小家电。更明智的购买决定让人们在经济上受益,而更少的消费也有助于保护环境。此外,当广告变得没那么重要时,企业就不得不专注于提高产品和服务的质量,而不是在宣传上花大笔钱,这对每个人都有好处。

总之,对广告更清醒的认识和社交媒体的广泛使用,降低了广告在人们选择中的作用,我认为这是一个积极的趋势。

Compared with the past, advertisements now have much less influence on many consumers. This change reflects how people understand advertising and make purchasing decisions, and I consider it a positive development.

The main reason why advertising has become less influential is that many consumers now realise its only aim is to sell products. They also understand that the items shown in adverts are often made to look better than they really are in order to tempt people to buy. With this understanding, it is natural for them to pay less attention to advertisements. In addition, social media has become a platform where people share their experiences of products and services, and many consumers now search for this information before buying. For example, before buying a new phone, many people read user reviews on Amazon or Xiaohongshu rather than trusting the advert. Often, it is these reviews, not advertisements, that people consider more reliable.

I would argue that this development benefits both consumers and society. For consumers, it saves money that would otherwise be spent on things they do not really need. For instance, people who ignore advertisements will not be persuaded to buy the latest smartphone or kitchen gadget when their old one still works well. Wiser purchasing decisions benefit people financially, and less consumption also helps to protect the environment. Besides, when advertising matters less, companies are forced to focus on improving the quality of their products and services instead of spending huge sums on publicity, which benefits everyone.

In conclusion, a better understanding of advertising and the widespread use of social media have reduced the role advertising plays in people's choices, and I believe this is a positive trend.
