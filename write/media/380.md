## 题目

More and more people read news from the Internet, but newspapers will continue to be read by the majority of people. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:报纸不如网络方便、更新慢;只有少数人(主要是老年人)还会读。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,即使现在越来越多的人在网上看新闻,将来大多数人仍然会读报纸。我不同意这个观点,因为在方便程度和速度方面,报纸都无法和网络媒体竞争。

我不相信大多数人会继续读报纸,主要原因是报纸远不如数字媒体方便。过去,人们如果想在外面获取信息,就得买一份报纸随身带着。然而,有了能上网的智能手机或平板,无论身在何处,人们都能轻松地了解世界各地正在发生的事情。因此,他们没有必要去买一份纸质报纸。

另一个原因是,报纸最快也只能报道前一天的事情,这导致获取信息有延迟。因为报纸需要印刷并送到商店,所以报纸上的文章无法经常更新。相比之下,当世界上任何地方发生了什么事情,相关的报道几乎马上就会在网上发布。这意味着,比如说,住在欧洲的人几分钟之内就能了解亚洲发生的事情。

诚然,有些人,尤其是年纪大的读者,仍然喜欢一边喝早咖啡一边读纸质报纸。然而,这个群体正在变小,许多报纸已经减少了印刷版,或者转到了网上。

总之,我强烈反对大多数人会继续读报纸的观点。与网络新闻相比,报纸不够方便,传递信息也更慢。

Some people argue that newspapers will still be read by most people in the future, even though a growing number of people now read news online. I disagree with this idea, because newspapers cannot compete with online media in terms of convenience and speed.

The main reason why I do not believe that most people will continue to read newspapers is that they are far less convenient than digital media. In the past, people had to buy a newspaper and carry it with them if they wanted to get information while out. However, with a smartphone or tablet connected to the internet, people can easily find out what is happening around the world, wherever they are. Therefore, there is no need for them to buy a physical newspaper.

Another reason is that newspapers can only report events from the previous day at the earliest, which causes a delay in getting information. Because newspapers need to be printed and delivered to shops, their articles cannot be updated frequently. In contrast, when something happens anywhere in the world, reports on it are published online almost immediately. This means that people living in Europe, for example, can learn about events in Asia within minutes.

Admittedly, some people, especially older readers, still enjoy reading a printed newspaper with their morning coffee. However, this group is becoming smaller, and many newspapers have already reduced their printed editions or moved online.

In conclusion, I strongly disagree that newspapers will continue to be read by the majority of people. Compared with online news, newspapers are less convenient and slower to deliver information.
