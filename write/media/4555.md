## 题目

Many people use social media every day to get in touch with other people and news events. Do you think the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 弊大于利:新闻没经过核实、信息过载;网上交流质量低,削弱面对面交流的能力。

## 中文(用来翻译)

除了日常交流,社交媒体现在还被广泛用来传播新闻。因此,许多人能即时了解时事。尽管这很方便,但我认为过度使用社交媒体弊大于利。

诚然,社交媒体让公众比通过报纸等传统媒体更快地获得新闻。它也帮助人们交流和交换信息。例如,在新冠疫情期间,社交媒体证明了它可以代替家庭聚会和学校会议等线下活动。

然而,许多人太依赖社交媒体,以至于忽视了它的长期副作用。从社交媒体上获取新闻的主要问题是,信息往往在被核实之前就被分享了。每天,人们的信息流里都塞满了政治争论、自拍、链接和品牌视频。面对这样的信息过载,许多用户只是划过标题;而当他们真的去读一篇报道时,可能也分辨不出它是真是假。

认为社交媒体能改善交流的想法也过于简单了。网上的对话质量往往不高,因为通过文字消息很难感受到对方的情绪。此外,过于依赖社交媒体的人可能会失去进行深入的面对面交谈的能力,而这种交谈对建立亲密的关系很重要。因此,依赖社交媒体实际上可能会降低交流的质量。

总之,我认为用社交媒体进行日常联系和获取新闻的好处,比不上它长期的负面影响。因此,人们应该限制使用社交媒体,并通过可靠的来源来核实重要的新闻。

Besides daily communication, social media is now widely used to spread news. As a result, many people have instant access to current events. Despite this convenience, I believe that the excessive use of social media brings more disadvantages than advantages.

It is true that social media makes news available to the public faster than traditional media such as newspapers. It also helps people communicate and exchange information. During the COVID-19 pandemic, for example, social media proved its value as a replacement for offline activities, such as family gatherings and school meetings.

However, many people are so attached to social media that they overlook its long-term side effects. The major problem with getting news from social media is that information is often shared before it has been checked. Every day, people's news feeds are filled with political arguments, selfies, links and brand videos. Faced with this information overload, many users simply scroll past the headlines, and when they do read a story, they may not be able to tell whether it is true or fake.

The idea that social media improves communication is also too simple. Online conversations are often of poor quality, because it is hard to sense the other person's emotions through text messages. Moreover, people who rely too much on social media may lose the ability to have deep face-to-face conversations, which are important for building close relationships. Thus, depending on social media can actually reduce the quality of communication.

In conclusion, I believe that the benefits of using social media for daily contact and news are smaller than its long-term negative effects. People should therefore limit their use of it and check important news through reliable sources.
