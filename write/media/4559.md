## 题目

More and more people no longer read the newspaper or watch TV programs to get news. They get news about the world through the Internet. Is this a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 积极:网上看新闻更便宜、更快,内容更丰富,可以比较不同来源。

## 中文(用来翻译)

近几十年来,人们获取新闻的方式发生了很大的变化,从报纸和电视转向了互联网。我坚信这是一个积极的发展。

这一变化的主要好处之一是,现在看新闻更便宜、更快了。人们不再需要在家打开电视或者去商店买报纸;相反,他们几乎随时随地都能用智能手机或平板了解世界上正在发生的事情,无论是在上班的路上,还是睡前躺在床上。此外,网络新闻一写好就能发布,而不是像纸质报纸那样一天只出一次。新闻报道还会不断更新,所以读者可以很容易地跟进事件的发展。

网络新闻的另一个优点是内容丰富。人们可以比较不同的信息来源,这能帮助他们核实新闻是否真实,避免偏见和误解。例如,当发生重大事件时,人们可以阅读多个新闻网站的报道,观看目击者拍摄的视频,而不是只依赖一家可能遗漏或歪曲信息的新闻机构。此外,传统报纸和广播没有报道的一些较小的地方新闻,在网上也能找到。

总之,虽然网络新闻的兴起是以牺牲传统新闻行业为代价的,但我认为这一趋势有非常积极的影响,因为信息传播更高效,读者也能获得更丰富的内容。

In recent decades, the way people get news has changed greatly, moving from newspapers and television to the internet. I firmly believe this is a positive development.

One of the main benefits of this change is that news can now be read more cheaply and quickly. People no longer need to turn on the TV at home or buy a newspaper from a shop; instead, they can find out what is happening in the world by simply using their smartphones or tablets almost anytime and anywhere, whether on their way to work or in bed before sleep. In addition, online news can be published as soon as it is ready, rather than once a day like printed newspapers. Stories are also updated constantly, so readers can easily follow how an event develops.

Another advantage of online news is the wide range of content. People can compare different sources of information, which helps them check whether a story is true and avoid bias and misunderstanding. For instance, when a major event happens, people can read reports from several news websites and watch videos taken by witnesses, instead of relying on one news organisation that might leave out or distort information. Furthermore, smaller local stories that are not covered by traditional newspapers or broadcasts can also be found online.

In conclusion, although the rise of online news has come at the expense of the traditional news industry, I believe this trend has a very positive impact, because information spreads more efficiently and readers have access to a much wider range of content.
