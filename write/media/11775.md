## 题目

People these days prefer to watch films and TV programmes alone rather than with other people. Why is that? Do you think it is a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 原因是生活忙碌和流媒体、个人设备的普及;这是消极的发展,会削弱人际关系、限制视野。

## 中文(用来翻译)

家人或朋友经常聚在一起看电影或电视节目的日子已经一去不复返了;如今,许多人更喜欢一个人享受娱乐。这一趋势可以用生活方式的改变和娱乐内容传播方式的改变来解释,我认为这是一个消极的发展。

这种转变的主要原因是人们的日程变得更忙了。过去,父母常常回家比较早,有更多时间和孩子一起看电视。如今,许多人工作时间更长,作息也各不相同,所以很难找到全家人都有空的时间。一个人看更方便。另一个因素是流媒体平台和个人设备的兴起。有了奈飞(Netflix)这样的服务,手机和平板上有数不清的节目,每个人都能看自己喜欢的东西。例如,一个家庭成员喜欢的电影,其他人可能完全不感兴趣,这就让一起看变得没那么有吸引力了。

尽管有这些原因,我认为这一趋势弊大于利。一个主要的担忧是,人际关系可能会变弱。一起看电影让人们有机会聊天、交流看法、了解彼此的兴趣。当这些共同的经历消失后,家人之间有意义的对话可能会变少,这可能会造成情感上的距离。此外,一个人看可能会限制人们接触新的想法。当人们看别人推荐的东西时,他们更有可能发现新类型的电影、新的文化或新的观点。相比之下,那些总是按照自己的口味选节目的人,视野可能会变得更窄。

总之,人们喜欢一个人看电影和节目,是忙碌的现代生活方式和个人流媒体服务造成的。然而,我认为这是一个消极的发展,因为它可能会削弱人际关系,限制人们接触新的想法。

Gone are the days when families or friends regularly gathered to watch films or television programmes together; nowadays, many people prefer to enjoy entertainment alone. This trend can be explained by changes in lifestyles and in the way entertainment is delivered, and I believe it is a negative development.

The main reason for this shift is that people's schedules have become busier. In the past, parents often came home earlier and had more time to watch television with their children. Today, many people work longer hours and have different routines, so it is difficult to find a time when the whole family is free. Watching alone is simply more convenient. Another factor is the rise of streaming platforms and personal devices. With services such as Netflix and countless programmes available on phones and tablets, each person can watch exactly what they like. For example, a film that one family member enjoys may not interest the others at all, which makes watching together less attractive.

Despite these explanations, I believe the trend has more negative effects than benefits. One major concern is that relationships may become weaker. Watching films together gives people chances to talk, exchange opinions and understand each other's interests. When these shared experiences disappear, family members may have fewer meaningful conversations, which could create emotional distance. Furthermore, watching alone may limit people's exposure to new ideas. When people watch something recommended by others, they are more likely to discover new kinds of films, cultures or viewpoints. In contrast, those who always choose programmes based on their own tastes may develop a narrower outlook.

In conclusion, the preference for watching films and programmes alone is the result of busy modern lifestyles and personal streaming services. Nevertheless, I consider it a negative development because it may weaken relationships and limit people's exposure to new ideas.
