## 题目

Some people argue that the content children watch on television affects their behaviour, while others believe the amount of time they spend watching television is the influencing factor. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 内容和时间都有影响,但真正的问题是缺少家长的引导。

## 中文(用来翻译)

电视对孩子行为的影响是一个一直有争议的话题。有些人认为孩子看的节目类型是关键因素,另一些人则认为看电视的时间更重要。本文将先讨论这两种观点,然后给出我自己的看法。

支持第一种观点的人认为,节目的内容会强烈地影响孩子的行为。他们认为,暴力或带有攻击性的节目会让孩子做出类似的行为,因为孩子常常模仿他们看到的东西。例如,经常看打斗动画片的小孩可能会在操场上打同学。此外,展示不切实际的身材形象的节目可能会伤害孩子的自尊心,影响他们看待世界的方式。

另一方面,关注看电视时间的人认为,无论内容是什么,看屏幕的时间太长都会产生负面影响。医生警告说,看太多电视会导致缺乏运动的生活方式,这可能引起肥胖和相关的健康问题。同样,许多老师认为,看电视会占用其他重要活动的时间,比如户外玩耍、阅读和与朋友相处,而这些活动对孩子的成长是必不可少的。

在我看来,内容和时间都会影响孩子的行为,但真正的问题是缺少家长的引导。家长不仅应该检查孩子在看什么,比如挑选教育类节目和纪录片,还应该限制他们看电视的时间,鼓励他们平衡地参加各种活动。

总之,电视节目的内容和看电视的时间都会影响孩子的行为。然而,最重要的因素是用心的教养方式来管理这种影响。

The influence of television on children's behaviour is a subject of constant debate. Some people believe that the type of programmes children watch is the key factor, while others argue that the amount of time they spend watching matters more. This essay will discuss both views before giving my own opinion.

Supporters of the first view argue that the content of programmes strongly shapes children's behaviour. They believe that violent or aggressive programmes can lead children to behave in a similar way, because children often copy what they see. For example, young children who watch cartoons full of fighting may hit their classmates in the playground. Furthermore, programmes that show unrealistic body images can damage children's self-esteem and their view of the world.

On the other hand, those who focus on viewing time argue that, whatever the content, too much screen time can have negative effects. Doctors warn that watching too much television can lead to an inactive lifestyle, which may cause obesity and related health problems. Likewise, many teachers believe that it takes away time from other important activities, such as outdoor play, reading and spending time with friends, which are essential for children's development.

In my view, both the content and the amount of viewing can affect children's behaviour, but the real problem is a lack of parental guidance. Parents should not only check what their children are watching, such as choosing educational programmes and documentaries, but also limit how long they watch, encouraging a balance of different activities.

In conclusion, both the content of television programmes and the time spent watching them can shape children's behaviour. However, the most important factor is careful parenting to manage this influence.
