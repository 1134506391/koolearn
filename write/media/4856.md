## 题目

Some people think visual images like photographs or videos can tell information more accurately in a news story, while others think they are not reliable sources. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 照片和视频有价值,但可能被断章取义或篡改,要谨慎看待、多方核实。

## 中文(用来翻译)

有人认为,新闻报道里的照片和视频能更准确地告诉人们发生了什么,另一些人则认为这类素材不可靠。本文将讨论这两种观点,我的看法是:视觉图像在新闻报道中很有价值,但人们仍然应该批判性地看待它们。

有人认为视觉图像能帮助人们更准确地理解新闻,这是可以理解的。照片和视频直接展示事件,所以观众能很快看到发生了什么、在哪里发生、涉及哪些人。相比之下,文字报道可能包含一些读者看不懂的难词或表达。此外,图像能捕捉到文字无法完全描述的细节。例如,地震的视频画面比文字描述能更清楚地展示破坏的规模。

然而,另一些人认为新闻里的照片和视频并不总是可信的。第一,图像可能被断章取义。比如,一张抗议人群的照片经过裁剪,可以让人群看起来比实际多得多或少得多。第二,有了现代的编辑软件和人工智能,修改图像甚至制作看起来完全真实的假视频已经变得很容易。这意味着,即使是在现场拍摄的画面,也可能在发布前被修改过。

总之,我认为双方的观点都有道理。总的来说,视觉图像能帮助人们理解新闻报道,但应该谨慎对待。因此,人们应该看看这些图像是否来自可靠的新闻机构,并在下结论之前比较多个来源。

Some people believe that photographs and video clips in news reports tell people about events more accurately, while others think such material is unreliable. This essay will discuss both views, and I will argue that visual images are valuable in news reports, but people should still view them critically.

It is understandable why some people think visual images help people understand news stories more accurately. Photographs and videos show events in a direct way, so viewers can quickly see what happened, where it happened and who was involved. Written articles, by contrast, may contain difficult words or expressions that some readers do not understand. Moreover, images can capture details that words cannot fully describe. For example, video footage of an earthquake shows the scale of the damage far more clearly than a written description.

However, others argue that photos and videos in the news cannot always be trusted. Firstly, images can be shown out of context. A photo of a small group of protesters, for instance, can be cropped to make the crowd look much larger or smaller than it really was. Secondly, with modern editing software and artificial intelligence, it has become easy to change images or even create fake videos that look completely real. This means that even footage recorded on the spot may be changed before it is published.

In conclusion, I believe that both sides of the argument are reasonable. On balance, visual images can help people understand news reports, but they should be treated with caution. Therefore, people should check whether the images come from a reliable news organisation and compare several sources before drawing conclusions.
