## 题目

The media should include more stories that report good news. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

**立场(一句话):** 不同意:新闻最重要的是真实、全面,好消息有积极作用,但不应该刻意多报。

## 中文(用来翻译)

在现代社会,包括电视、互联网和报纸在内的媒体变得越来越有影响力。有人建议媒体应该多报道好消息,但我不同意这个观点。

新闻最重要的品质是准确和全面。媒体的主要工作是报道事实,无论是正面的还是负面的。如果媒体只突出好消息,忽视甚至掩盖问题,公众可能会变得过于自信,无法为未来的困难做好准备。例如,如果关于人工智能的报道只关注新的机会和技术进步,而忽视了失业率上升的风险,许多工人可能会错过在失去工作之前学习新技能的机会。同样,如果环境问题很少被报道,政府解决这些问题的压力也会变小。

诚然,报道好消息在营造积极的社会氛围方面起着重要作用。关于航天技术成就、体育比赛胜利或者普通市民救人的报道,能让人们为自己的国家感到自豪,并鼓励他们做好事。然而,过分关注光明的一面是不明智的,因为它会让人产生一切都很好的错觉。好消息发生时就应该报道,但不应该仅仅因为它让人开心就被挑选出来报道。

总之,我认为媒体应该专注于公正、准确地报道真相,而不是刻意多报道好消息。只有当人们既了解社会的成就,也了解社会的问题时,他们才能做出明智的决定。

The media, including television, the internet and newspapers, have become increasingly powerful in modern society. Some people suggest that they should report more good news, but I disagree with this view.

The most important qualities of news are accuracy and completeness. The main job of the media is to report facts, whether they are positive or negative. If the media only highlight good news and ignore or hide problems, the public may become overconfident and fail to prepare for future difficulties. For example, if reports about artificial intelligence focus only on new opportunities and technological progress, while ignoring the risk of rising unemployment, many workers may lose the chance to learn new skills before their jobs disappear. Similarly, if environmental problems are rarely reported, governments may face less pressure to deal with them.

Admittedly, reporting good news plays a valuable role in creating a positive social atmosphere. Stories about achievements in space technology, sporting victories or ordinary citizens rescuing others can make people feel proud of their country and encourage them to behave well. However, paying too much attention to the bright side is unwise, because it can create a false sense that everything is fine. Good news should be reported when it happens, but it should not be chosen simply because it makes people feel happy.

In conclusion, I believe the media should focus on reporting the truth fairly and accurately, rather than deliberately including more good news. Only when people understand both the successes and the problems of society can they make sensible decisions.
